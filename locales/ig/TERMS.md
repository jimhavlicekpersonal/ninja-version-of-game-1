# Asụsụ Igbo: okwu ndị a họọrọ

Igbo Izugbe (Standard Igbo), Naịjirịa. Mkpụrụedemede ntụpọ: ị ọ ụ na ṅ (NFC). Anaghị ede akara ụda (tone marks) ma ọ bụrụ na ọ dị mkpa iji kewaa okwu abụọ. A na-akpọ onye egwuregwu **gị** (otu onye, enweghị nwoke/nwanyị). Igbo enweghị okike ụtọasụsụ; nnọchiaha "ọ/o" anaghị egosi nwoke ma ọ bụ nwanyị, ya mere ọ dị mma maka onye egwuregwu na `{name}`. Bọtịn bụ ngwaa iwu dị mkpụmkpụ (Wuo, Kwalite, Nara).

Rules (for reviewers):
- Noun before its modifier and its owner: `obodo {name}`, `Kwalite {building}`. Nothing is attached to a placeholder.
- Numbers follow the noun: `ndị dike {n}`, `ụlọ {n}`, `ugboro {n}`, `kpakpando {n}`. Plural has only `other` (Intl.PluralRules('ig')).
- Times: `{d}ụb` (ụbọchị), `{h}aw` (awa), `{m}nk` (nkeji), `{s}sk` (sekọnd).
- Level: `ọkwa {level}` (English "Lv").

Brand and technical names stay as they are (Kagemura, App Store, Google Play, Game Center, Play Games, IndexedDB, Sentry). Japanese names keep their usual Latin spelling (Kaito, Takeda, Gorō); English epithets are translated.

## Generated names

Recommended `generatedNames`: **`noun-first`** (second part, space, first part). Igbo puts the head noun first and its modifier or associated noun after it (ugwu egbe eluigwe = "peak of thunder"). Second parts are nouns (Ugwu, Ọnụ Ụzọ, Ndagwurugwu), first parts are nouns or invariant modifiers that fit after any of them (Egbe Eluigwe, Igwe, Alụlụ): Ugwu Egbe Eluigwe, Ọnụ Ụzọ Igwe. Nothing agrees in gender, so every pair reads naturally.

Onye na-asụ Igbo dịka asụsụ obodo ya enyochabeghị ntụgharị a.
