# isiZulu: amagama akhethiwe

isiZulu esijwayelekile (eNingizimu Afrika). Umdlali ubizwa ngo-"wena" (ubunye, ngobungane): imiyalo isebunyeni (Yakha, Thuthukisa, Qoqa). isiZulu asinabo ubulili, kodwa izakhi zamagama (noun-class prefixes) nezivumelwano (concords) zincike egameni: akukho sakhi esinamathiselwa ku-placeholder ({name}, {building}, {troop}), futhi akukho senzo esinesivumelwano somenzi esincike ku-placeholder. Sisebenzisa ikholoni (`Kuqediwe: {building}`), isenzo esingenamuntu (ku-), noma igama elinesakhi esaziwayo ngaphambi kwe-placeholder (`isakhiwo {building}`). Izinombolo: isivumelwano sincike ebizweni elaziwayo (`amaqhawe angu-#`, `izinkanyezi ezingu-#`).

Amagama aseJapane agcinwa ngesiLatini (Kaito, Takeda, Gorō). Iziqu zabantu (epithets) ziyahunyushwa.

## Amagama enziwayo (generatedNames: `noun-first`)

Ibizo kuqala, bese isichasiso, kuhlukaniswe ngesikhala: `Intaba` + `yeNdudumo` = *Intaba yeNdudumo*. Wonke amabizo engxenye yesibili (`names.placeSecond`, `social.groveNames.second`) asesigabeni 9 (in-/im-), ngakho zonke izingxenye zokuqala zisebenzisa izivumelwano zesigaba 9 (ya-/ye-/yo- wobunikazi, e- wesibaluli): yonke ingxenye yokuqala ifaneleka nayo yonke ingxenye yesibili.

## Ubuningi

`Intl.PluralRules('zu')`: `one` (0 no-1) ne-`other`.
