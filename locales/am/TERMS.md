# አማርኛ፦ የተመረጡ ቃላት (Amharic: chosen terms)

Standard Amharic (am-ET) in Ge'ez script, NFC. The player is addressed with the polite, gender-neutral form: እርስዎ, possessive -ዎ (መንደርዎ), and plural-of-respect imperatives (ይገንቡ, ያሻሽሉ, ይቀበሉ, ይሞክሩ); never the masculine/feminine singular imperatives (ገንባ/ገንቢ). A player or `{name}` in the third person takes plural/honorific verb forms (አሸንፈዋል, ተቀላቅለዋል), which state no gender. Sensei Hoshi says እኔ and addresses the player as እርስዎ.

Ethiopic punctuation inside sentences: ። (full stop), ፣ (comma), ፦ (label colon: `ዝግጁ፦ {building}`), ? and ! as usual. Nothing (የ, በ, ለ, ከ, -ን, -ም) is attached directly to a placeholder or `#`: use a colon or a noun beside the placeholder.

Brands and technical names (Kagemura, App Store, Google Play, Game Center, Play Games, IndexedDB, Sentry) stay in Latin letters.

Japanese names are transliterated into Ge'ez script (Kaito ካይቶ, Hana ሃና, Takeda ታኬዳ). English epithets are translated.

Plurals: `one` (0 and 1) and `other`. A number before a noun keeps the noun singular where natural (`{n} ተዋጊ`, `{n} ሕንፃ`, `{n} ጊዜ`, `{n} ኮከብ`). Times: `{h} ሰዓት {m} ደቂቃ`.

Generated names: the default setting (first part, space, second part), because Amharic puts the modifier before the noun (የነጎድጓድ + ጫፍ = የነጎድጓድ ጫፍ). First parts are genitive phrases with የ or invariant adjectives that fit any noun; second parts are plain nouns. No `generatedNames` value is needed.

This translation has not been reviewed by a native Amharic speaker.
