# Hausa: kalmomin da aka zaɓa

Hausa na yau da kullum (Boko, haruffan ɓ ɗ ƙ ƴ), Najeriya da Nijar. Ana kiran ɗan wasa da **ku** (jam'i na girmamawa), domin "ka/ki" na nuna jinsi: ku, kuna, kun, naku, -ku (ƙauyenku). Maɓallai suna amfani da umurni marar jinsi (Gina, Inganta, Karɓa).

Rules (for reviewers):
- Never *ka/ki/kai/ke* for the player, and never *ya/ta/shi/ita* (he/she) for a player, `{name}` or another player: rephrase with a noun (`Nasara: {name}`), an impersonal *an* sentence, or *ɗan wasa / abokin hamayya*. The game's own fixed characters (Akane, Yoru, Gorō) may take *ta/ya*.
- No adjective or linker that depends on the gender of a placeholder: a linker attaches only to a fixed noun before it (`ƙauyen {name}`, `ginin {building}`), never to the placeholder.
- Plurals: `one` / `other` (Intl.PluralRules('ha')). The noun comes before the number: `{n, plural, one {mayaƙi #} other {mayaƙa #}}`.
- Times: `{d}kw` (kwana), `{h}a` (awa), `{m}m` (minti), `{s}d` (daƙiƙa).

Brand and technical names stay as they are (Kagemura, App Store, Google Play, Game Center, Play Games, Sentry). Japanese names keep their usual Latin spelling (Kaito, Takeda, Gorō).

## Generated names

Recommended `generatedNames`: **`noun-first`** (second part, space, first part). Hausa puts the head noun first and joins it to the next noun with the genitive linker *-n/-r*, which follows the head noun's gender. The second parts therefore carry their own linker (Dutsen, Ƙofar, Kwarin), and the first parts are plain nouns that fit after any of them (Tsawa, Ƙarfe, Hazo): Dutsen Tsawa, Ƙofar Ƙarfe, Kwarin Hazo. With the default order (first, space, second) the names would read backwards.

Wannan fassara ba ta sami bitar ɗan asalin harshen Hausa ba tukuna.
