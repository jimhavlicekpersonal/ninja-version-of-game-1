Xhumana nathi

I-imeyili:

Uma udlala ku-inthanethi, faka i-ID yakho yomdlali (Izilungiselelo → Ukudlala ku-inthanethi → I-akhawunti) futhi, ekuthengeni, usuku nenombolo ye-oda yesitolo. Ungalokothi uthumele ikhodi yakho yokulondoloza noma iphasiwedi yanoma ubani.

Isigodi sami silahlekile noma sibukeka singalungile

  • Isigodi sakho silondolozwe kule divayisi. Uma igeyimu ingakwazanga ukufunda okulondoloziwe, iyakugcina: bheka Izigodi zangaphambili ku-Izilungiselelo bese uthinta u-Buyisa.
  • Ukuze uthuthele isigodi kwenye idivayisi, sebenzisa Izilungiselelo → Kopisha ikhodi yokulondoloza kudivayisi endala kanye no-Layisha ngekhodi kwentsha. Isigodi esigcinwe kuseva yethu siyabuya lapho ungena kudivayisi entsha ngendlela yokungena oyixhumanisile (Izilungiselelo → Ukudlala ku-inthanethi → I-akhawunti → Izindlela zokungena).
  • Uma igeyimu ithi isigodi sivuliwe kwenye indawo, vala enye ithebhu noma i-app: yileyo evulwe okokugcina kuphela elondolozayo.

Ukuthenga

  • Uma ukuthenga kungafikanga, vula Isitolo bese uthinta u-Buyisa ukuthenga ngenkathi uxhunyiwe. Ukuthenga okulinde ukugunyazwa ngumzali noma yisitolo kufika lapho sekugunyaziwe.
  • Ukubuyiswa kwemali kusingathwa yi-Apple noma yi-Google: bacele ngomlando wakho wokuthenga we-App Store noma we-Google Play. Uma isitolo sibuyisela imali yokuthenga, lokho okwanikwa kungathathwa esigodini esigcinwe kuseva yethu.
  • Abadlali abangaphansi kweminyaka eyi-18 banomkhawulo wokusebenzisa imali wanyanga zonke. Uma uthe ungaphansi kweminyaka eyi-18 ngephutha, sibhalele ne-ID yakho yomdlali: igeyimu ayikwazi ukukubuyisela kubantu abadala ngokwayo.
  • Uhlobo lwewebhu lwegeyimu alithengisi lutho.

Ukudlala ku-inthanethi

  • Akukho xhumano: ungaqhubeka wakha; uma isigodi sakho sigcinwe kuseva yethu, igeyimu igcina izenzo zakho futhi izithumele lapho usubuyele ku-inthanethi. Ukugasela abadlali bangempela, imifelandawonye, abangane nokuthenga kudinga uxhumano.
  • Kudingeka ukubuyekeza: faka uhlobo lwakamuva kusuka esitolo sakho, noma ulayishe kabusha ikhasi lewebhu.
  • Kumisiwe noma kuthulisiwe: i-akhawunti yephule imithetho yomphakathi. Igeyimu ikutshela isizathu nokuthi kuze kube nini. Ukuze ukhalaze, sibhalele ne-ID yakho yomdlali kungakapheli izinyanga eziyisithupha: umuntu ongazange athathe isinqumo sokuqala uyakubheka futhi.
  • Iqembu leminyaka: igeyimu ibuza unyaka wokuzalwa kanye ngaphambi kokudlala ku-inthanethi futhi igcina iqembu leminyaka kuphela. Abadlali abangaphansi kweminyaka eyi-13 baxoxa ngemisho engaguquki kuphela, bathola igama elenziwe futhi abakwazi ukutholwa ngegama; abadlali abaneminyaka eyi-13 kuya kweyi-17 baqala befihliwe kwabangabazi futhi isimo sabo se-inthanethi sivaliwe. Igeyimu ingenza kuphela iqembu leminyaka liqine kakhulu; bazali, sibhaleleni uma ingane yenu inikeze iminyaka engalungile.

Kukhona ongihluphayo

Thinta ⋯ eduze komlayezo wakhe engxoxweni yoMfelandawonye noma yeviyo, ohlwini lwamalungu oMfelandawonye noma ekhadini lomngane ukuze Ubike noma Uvimbe. Ukuvimba kufihla ingxoxo yakhe nezicelo zakhe kuwe ngokushesha. Sikutshela ukuthi sinqume ini ngombiko wakho, futhi asisoze samtshela omunye umdlali ukuthi ubani omubikile. Bheka imithetho yomphakathi. Uma umuntu esengozini, qala uxhumane nezinsizakalo eziphuthumayo zendawo yakho.

Susa i-akhawunti yami

Kugeyimu: Izilungiselelo → Ukudlala ku-inthanethi → I-akhawunti → Susa i-akhawunti ye-inthanethi. Ngaphandle kwegeyimu: , ne-ID yakho yomdlali noma igama kanye nekhodi yokususa: yithole ku-Izilungiselelo → Ukudlala ku-inthanethi → I-akhawunti ngenkathi usenayo igeyimu (isebenza kanye, izinsuku ezingu-30). Ngaphandle kwekhodi kufanele siqale sihlole ukuthi i-akhawunti ingeyakho. Isigodi sakho kudivayisi siyahamba lapho ukhipha i-app noma usula idatha yesayithi. Imininingwane iku-nqubomgomo yobumfihlo.
