Ngamafuphi

  • Ngaphandle kokudlala ku-inthanethi, isigodi sakho sihlala kudivayisi yakho futhi igeyimu ayisithumeleli lutho ngawe (ngaphandle uma uvula imibiko yokuphahlazeka).
  • Ekudlaleni ku-inthanethi sigcina lokho igeyimu ekudingayo: i-akhawunti ene-ID yomdlali engahleliwe, igama olikhethayo, isigodi sakho nezimpi, ingxoxo, abangane nokuthenga.
  • Asiwuceli ikheli lakho le-imeyili, igama lakho langempela, oxhumana nabo noma indawo okuyo kugeyimu. Azikho izikhangiso, akukho ukulandelelwa kwamanye ama-app, futhi asisoze sayithengisa idatha yakho.
  • Ungasusa i-akhawunti yakho ye-inthanethi kugeyimu noma kuwebhu noma nini.

Amaqembu eminyaka nabadlali abasebancane

I-Kagemura iyigeyimu yamasu enempi yopopayi, ekalwe kwabasebasha nangaphezulu ezitolo zama-app. Ngaphambi kokuthi udlale ku-inthanethi, igeyimu ibuza unyaka wakho wokuzalwa (nenyanga, kuphela uma unyaka wodwa ungakwanele) ngaphandle kokuphakamisa impendulo, noma isebenzise ibanga leminyaka elinikezwa yisitolo sama-app sedivayisi yakho. Igcina kuphela iqembu leminyaka eliphumayo (ngaphansi kweminyaka eyi-13, eyi-13 kuya kweyi-17, noma eyi-18 nangaphezulu), hhayi usuku lwakho lokuzalwa, futhi ilisebenzisa kuphela ukulungisa igeyimu ngokweminyaka yakho:

  • Ngaphansi kweminyaka eyi-13: akukho ngxoxo yombhalo okhululekile (kuphela imisho engaguquki efana nethi "Inhlanhla enhle!"), igama lomdlali elenziwe esikhundleni salelo elikhethiwe, futhi abanye abadlali abakwazi ukukuthola ngegama. Usengadlala, ugasele futhi ujoyine uMfelandawonye.
  • Eyi-13 kuya kweyi-17: abangane babangane bakho kuphela abangakuthola ngegama, futhi abangane ababoni ukuthi wagcina nini ukuba ku-inthanethi, uze uzishintshe lezi ku-Izilungiselelo.
  • Ngaphansi kweminyaka eyi-18 (womabili amaqembu amancane): umkhawulo wokusebenzisa imali wanyanga zonke, isinyathelo esengeziwe sokuthi "buza umuntu omdala" ngaphambi kokuthenga, futhi ayikho iminikelo yesikhathi esilinganiselwe evela ngokwayo.

Iqembu leminyaka liyisitatimende sakho, hhayi ukuqinisekiswa kweminyaka. Uma usushilo ukuthi ungaphansi kweminyaka eyi-18, igeyimu ayikwazi ukukubuyisela kubantu abadala ngokwayo; ithimba lethu losizo lingakwenza, ngemuva kokuhlola. Uma ungumzali futhi ukholwa ukuthi ingane yakho inikeze iminyaka engalungile noma yabelana ngemininingwane yomuntu siqu, sibhalele futhi sizokusiza, kuhlanganise nokususa i-akhawunti. Lokhu kuvikelwa kusebenza ezinguqulweni zegeyimu ezikhishwe nakho; izinguqulo ezindala zibuza iqembu leminyaka kuphela ngaphambi kokuthenga kokuqala.

Ukudlala ngaphandle kokudlala ku-inthanethi

Isigodi sakho silondolozwe kudivayisi yakho. Igeyimu ayidingi i-akhawunti, futhi uma ukudlala ku-inthanethi kuvaliwe ayisithumeleli lutho ngawe (ngaphandle uma uvula imibiko yokuphahlazeka, ngezansi). Izibalo zokudlala ezikhonjiswa ku-Izilungiselelo ("Imicimbi yezibalo") zihlala kudivayisi, ngaphandle uma ungumuntu omdala odlala ku-inthanethi futhi ukhetha ukwabelana ngazo (ngezansi). Uma udlala esipheqululini sewebhu, iwebhusayithi eletha igeyimu ibona ikheli lakho le-IP, njengoba yenza yonke iwebhusayithi.

Lokho esikuqoqayo, ukuthi kungani, nangasiphi isisekelo somthetho

Izisekelo zomthetho yilezo ze-EU ne-UK GDPR (Isig. 6) kanye ne-LGPD yaseBrazil (Isig. 7). "Inkontileka" isho ukuthi sidinga idatha ukuze sinikeze igeyimu ocele ukuyidlala; "izithakazelo ezisemthethweni" kusho ukuthi silinganise isizathu sethu namalungelo akho futhi ungaphikisa (bheka "Amalungelo akho").

I-akhawunti ye-inthanethi: i-ID yomdlali engahleliwe, ithokheni yokungena eyimfihlo (sigcina kuphela i-hash yendlela eyodwa), igama olikhethayo, ikhodi yakho yomngane, iqembu lakho leminyaka, izinqumo zakho zobumfihlo, ukuthi wagcina nini ukuba ku-inthanethiUkuqhuba i-akhawunti yakho ye-inthanethi, ukukhombisa igama lakho kwabanye abadlali nokusebenzisa ukuvikelwa kweqembu lakho leminyaka. Isisekelo: inkontileka; eqenjini leminyaka futhi isibopho somthetho nezithakazelo ezisemthethweni (ukuvikela abadlali abasebancane). LGPD: ukufeza inkontileka, isibopho somthetho.
Igeyimu yakho: inqaba yakho yokugaselwa, isigodi esigcinwe kuseva yethu nezenzo ozenzayo kuso, izimpi nokuphinda, udumo, izinga nezilinganiso, umlando wezinsiza ezengeziwe noma ezisetshenzisiweUkuze ukudlala ku-inthanethi kusebenze, ukugasela kuhlolwe futhi kube nobulungiswa, futhi ukwazi ukuqhubeka kwenye idivayisi. Isisekelo: inkontileka; ukuhlola izimpi ukuthi akukho ukukopela kuyisithakazelo esisemthethweni.

Izindlela zokungena ozixhumanisayo (akuphoqelekile): i-id i-Apple, i-Google noma i-Game Center esinika yona ngawe; ku-Ngena nge-Apple futhi nethokheni i-Apple eyikhiphela igeyimu; ukuthi yiphi indlela yokungena idivayisi yakho ngayinye eyisebenzisile nokuthi yagcina nini ukusetshenziswaUkubuyisa i-akhawunti yakho kwenye idivayisi, ukukhipha amadivayisi lapho unqamula indlela yokungena, nokuhoxisa ukufinyelela kwegeyimu ku-Apple lapho ususa i-akhawunti yakho noma unqamula i-Apple. Asisoze samukela noma sagcina ikheli lakho le-imeyili noma igama lakho elivela kubo. Isisekelo: inkontileka, ngesicelo sakho.
Ukuthenga: i-id yokuthengiselana yesitolo, umkhiqizo, intengo, usuku nesimo sokubuyiswa kwemaliUkuletha ukuthenga ngakunye kanye, ukukubuyisa, ukusingatha ukubuyiswa kwemali nokusebenzisa umkhawulo wabangaphansi kweminyaka eyi-18. Uma ucela igeyimu ukuthi ithole abangane bakho be-Game Center, sisebenzisa ama-id abo kanye ukuze sibone ukuthi yibaphi abadlala i-Kagemura; asilugcini lolo hlu. Asenzi izinqumo ngawe ngezindlela ezizenzakalelayo ezinemiphumela yomthetho noma ebalulekile ngendlela efanayo. Kukhona isinyathelo esisodwa sokuphepha esizenzakalelayo: umlayezo wengxoxo obikwe ngabadlali abathathu abahlukene uyafihlwa kuze kube yilapho abasebenzi bethu bewubheka.

Izibalo zokudlala (kuvaliwe ngaphandle uma uthi yebo; abantu abadala kuphela)

Uma udlala ku-inthanethi futhi usitshelile ukuthi ungumuntu omdala, igeyimu ibuza kanye ukuthi ufuna yini ukwabelana ngezibalo zokudlala; Izilungiselelo zinenkinobho efanayo. Kuvaliwe uze uthi yebo, futhi igeyimu ayisoze yabuza, noma ithumele izibalo, kubadlali abangaphansi kweminyaka eyi-18 noma abangaziwa iminyaka yabo. Uma kuvuliwe, igeyimu ithumela lokho okwenzayo kugeyimu (izikhathi zokudlala eziqalayo, izinyathelo zesifundo, ukugasela nezinkanyezi zakho nokubhujiswa, ukuthuthukisa nocwaningo oluqalisiwe, iminikelo ekhonjisiwe, ijeyidi elisetshenziselwe ukusheshisa nokuthenga nomkhiqizo wakho nentengo) nge-id yezibalo engahleliwe eyenzelwe le njongo. I-id yezibalo akuyona i-ID yakho yomdlali futhi ayisoze yathunyelwa kanye nayo; igeyimu ayengezi igama lakho, isigodi, ingxoxo noma ithokheni yokungena, futhi iseva yethu ayiligcini ikheli le-IP kanye nazo. Ukuthi yebo kuphinde kuthumele lokho le divayisi ekuqophe ngaphambili (ukuze sibone imizuzu yokuqala yokudlala). Ukuvala inkinobho kususa lokho leyo id yezibalo ekuthumele; ukuthi yebo futhi kuqala entsha. Sigcina izibalo okwabelwane ngazo izinsuku ezingu-400 futhi sizisebenzisa kuphela ezibalweni eziphelele (isibonelo, mangaki abadlali abasha ababuye ngosuku olulandelayo). Abadlali ababelana ngazo bangathola izikhathi ezihluke kancane zeminikelo yangaphakathi kwegeyimu, ukuze kuqhathaniswe ukuthi yikuphi okusebenza kangcono; amanani, imiklomelo nemithetho yegeyimu kuyafana kubo bonke.

Imibiko yokuphahlazeka (kuvaliwe ngaphandle uma ukuvula)

Izinguqulo zegeyimu ezinokubika ukuphahlazeka zikhombisa inkinobho ethi "Thumela imibiko yokuphahlazeka" ku-Izilungiselelo. Kuvaliwe uze ukuvule. Uma kuvuliwe futhi igeyimu ihlangabezana nephutha, igeyimu ithumela umlayezo wephutha, lapho lenzeke khona kukhodi yegeyimu, uhlobo lwegeyimu nenkundla kusevisi yethu yokulandelela amaphutha (Sentry). Igeyimu ayinamathiseli igama lakho, i-ID yomdlali, isigodi, ingxoxo noma okulondoloziwe, futhi isusa amapharamitha amakheli ewebhu nanoma yini ebukeka njengekheli le-imeyili emlayezweni. Umlayezo wephutha usengaqukatha ngezikhathi ezithile umbhalo igeyimu ebiwusebenza lapho ihluleka. Isevisi ibona ikheli le-IP umbiko ovela kulo; siyisethile isevisi ukuthi ingawagcini amakheli e-IP. Ukuvala inkinobho kumisa imibiko emisha ngokushesha.

Imibiko, ukulawula nezaziso

Uma ubika umlayezo noma umdlali, abasebenzi bethu bayakubuyekeza futhi bangasusa umlayezo, bathulise ingxoxo noma bamise i-akhawunti (bheka imithetho yomphakathi). Sitshela umdlali obikile ukuthi kwenzekeni, futhi sitshela umdlali lapho sisusa umlayezo wakhe, simthulisa noma simmisa, nesizathu nendlela yokukhalaza. Asimtsheli umdlali obikiwe ukuthi ubani omubikile. Sonke isenzo sabasebenzi siyaqoshwa ukuze izinqumo zihlolwe.

Uma uxhumana nathi

  • I-imeyili yosizo. Uma usibhalela, sithola ikheli lakho le-imeyili nalokho okuthumelayo, futhi sikusebenzisa kuphela ukukuphendula nokugcina irekhodi laleyo mpendulo.
  • Izicelo zokususa zewebhu. Ikhasi lokususa i-akhawunti licela i-ID yakho yomdlali, ikhodi yokususa igeyimu ekunika yona, nenothi ongalifaka. Alicelli ikheli le-imeyili. Sigcina isicelo nalokho esikwenzile ngaso njengerekhodi lokuthi sisingathiwe.

Lokho esingakwenzi

Asiwuceli ikheli lakho le-imeyili kugeyimu, futhi asiqoqi oxhumana nabo, indawo okuyo ngqo, izithombe noma i-id yezikhangiso. Azikho izikhangiso futhi akukho ukulandelelwa kwezinkampani zangaphandle, futhi asiyithengisi idatha yakho noma sabelane ngayo ngezikhangiso noma ngezikhangiso zokuziphatha eziwela ezimweni ezihlukene.

Ubani oyicubungulayo, futhi kuphi

Sisebenzisa abahlinzeki bezinsizakalo abacubungula idatha kuphela ngemiyalo yethu: umhlinzeki wethu wokusingatha (iseva nesizindalwazi: ), isevisi yethu yokulandelela amaphutha i-Sentry (imibiko yokuphahlazeka kuphela uma uyivulile, namaphutha eseva yethu; isifunda sedatha: ), kanye ne-Apple ne-Google (ukungena, ukuthenga nezaziso, ngaphansi kwezinqubomgomo zabo zobumfihlo). Abasebenzi bethu babona lokho abakudingayo ukuze baphendule usizo futhi balawule.

Abanye balaba bahlinzeki bangacubungula idatha ngaphandle kwezwe lakho, kuhlanganise nangaphandle kwe-EU, i-UK noma iBrazil. Lapho idatha iphuma e-EU noma e-UK iya ezweni elingenaso isinqumo sokwanela, sithembela ku-Standard Contractual Clauses ye-European Commission (kanye nesengezo se-UK noma i-International Data Transfer Agreement) noma enye indlela yokudlulisa umthetho oyivumelayo. Ungasicela ikhophi yezivikelo.

Ukususa i-akhawunti yakho

  • Kugeyimu: Izilungiselelo → Ukudlala ku-inthanethi → I-akhawunti → Susa i-akhawunti ye-inthanethi.
  • Kuwebhu, uma ungasakwazi ukuvula igeyimu: . Faka i-ID yakho yomdlali nekhodi yokususa: yicele ku-Izilungiselelo → Ukudlala ku-inthanethi → I-akhawunti ngenkathi usenayo igeyimu (isebenza kanye nezinsuku ezingu-30; ukucela futhi kuyayishintsha, futhi sigcina kuphela i-hash yayo yendlela eyodwa). Ngekhodi sifeza isicelo; ngaphandle kwayo, kufanele siqale siqiniseke ukuthi isicelo sivela kumnikazi we-akhawunti, ngoba ama-ID abadlali namagama ayabonakala kwabanye abadlali. Sifeza izicelo kungakapheli izinsuku ezingu-30.
  • Ukususa kususa i-akhawunti yakho ye-inthanethi, igama, inqaba, imilayezo yengxoxo, abangane, ubulungu beviyo, imibiko oyenzile, izindlela zokungena ezixhunyanisiwe nesigodi esigcinwe kuseva yethu. Uma uxhumanise u-Ngena nge-Apple, siphinde sicele i-Apple ukuthi ihoxise ukufinyelela kwegeyimu. Ukugasela okwenzile kuhlala ekuphindweni kwabanye abadlali njengo-"Umdlali osusiwe"; amarekhodi okuthenga ahlala ngaphandle kwe-ID yakho yomdlali (ngenhla).
  • Isigodi sakho kudivayisi sisuswa ngokukhipha i-app noma ngokusula idatha yesayithi esipheqululini sakho; Izilungiselelo → "Qala isigodi esisha" kusishintsha.

Amalungelo akho

Noma ngabe uhlala kuphi, ungasicela ukuthi:

  • ufinyelele idatha yakho futhi uthole ikhophi, futhi wamukele idatha osinike yona ngendlela esetshenziswa kakhulu, efundeka ngomshini (ukuthutheka);
  • uyilungise, noma uyisuse (bheka ngenhla);
  • ukhawulele indlela esiyisebenzisa ngayo ngenkathi umbuzo usaxazululwa, futhi uphikise ukusetshenziswa okusekelwe ezithakazelweni ezisemthethweni;
  • uhoxise imvume noma nini (imibiko yokuphahlazeka, izibalo zokudlala, izaziso: Izilungiselelo), ngaphandle kokuthinta okwenzeka ngaphambili. Ukuvala izibalo zokudlala kuphinde kususe lokho okwabelwane ngakho.

Bhalela ne-ID yakho yomdlali. Siphendula kungakapheli inyanga eyodwa (eBrazil, ngesikhathi esibekwe yi-LGPD; eCalifornia, kungakapheli izinsuku ezingu-45) futhi kungase kudingeke siqale siqinisekise ukuthi i-akhawunti ingeyakho. Ungakhalaza nakwisiphathimandla sokuvikelwa kwedatha: e-EU isiphathimandla salapho uhlala khona noma usebenza khona, e-UK i-Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk), eBrazil i-ANPD (gov.br/anpd). Izakhamuzi zaseCalifornia zinamalungelo e-CCPA/CPRA, kuhlanganise nelokwazi, lokususa, lokulungisa nelokungabandlululwa ngokuwasebenzisa; asithengisi noma sabelane ngolwazi lomuntu siqu njengoba leyo mithetho ilichaza. Ngaphansi kwe-LGPD ungacela nokuqinisekiswa kokuthi siyayicubungula idatha yakho, ukwenziwa kwayo ingaziwa, ulwazi mayelana nalabo esabelana nabo ngayo, nemiphumela yokwenqaba imvume.

Ukuthenga

Ukuthenga kwenziwa nge-App Store noma nge-Google Play. Bona basingatha imininingwane yakho yokukhokha; thina asisoze sayibona. Imigomo yesevisi ichaza ijeyidi nokunye ukuthenga.

Ukuphepha

Igeyimu ixhumana neseva yethu kuphela ngoxhumano olubethelwe (HTTPS), amathokheni okungena agcinwa njengama-hash endlela eyodwa, futhi ukufinyelela kwabasebenzi kunomkhawulo futhi kuyaqoshwa. Alukho uhlelo oluphephe ngokuphelele; uma ukwephulwa kukuthinta, sizokutshela wena neziphathimandla njengoba umthetho udinga.

Ushintsho

Siyalibuyekeza leli khasi nosuku lwalo lapho lokho esikuqoqayo kushintsha, futhi sitshele abadlali kugeyimu ngoshintsho olubalulekile.
