If you play online, include your Player ID (Settings → Online play → Account) and, for a purchase, the date and the store's order number. Never send your save code or anyone's password.

My village is gone or looks wrong

  • Your village is saved on this device. If the game could not read a save, it keeps it: look for Earlier villages in Settings and tap Restore.
  • To move a village to another device, use Settings → Copy save code on the old device and Load from code on the new one. A village kept on our server comes back when you sign in on the new device with a sign-in you linked (Settings → Online play → Account → Sign-ins).
  • If the game says the village is open somewhere else, close the other tab or app: only the one opened last saves.

Purchases

  • If a purchase did not arrive, open the Store and tap Restore purchases while online. A purchase waiting for a parent's approval or the store arrives when it is approved.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple or Google: ask them through your App Store or Google Play purchase history. When a store refunds a purchase, what it gave can be taken back from a village kept on our server.
  • Players under 18 have a monthly spending limit. If you said you are under 18 by mistake, write to us with your Player ID: the game cannot switch back to adult on its own.
  • The web version of the game sells nothing.
  • Jade has no cash value and does not expire; see the terms of service. For players in Japan: 特定商取引法に基づく表記 · 資金決済法に基づく表示.

Online play

  • No connection: you can keep building; if your village is kept on our server, the game keeps your actions and sends them when you are back online. Raids on real players, Alliances, friends and purchases need a connection.
  • Update needed: install the latest version from your store, or reload the web page.
  • Suspended or muted: the account broke the community rules. The game tells you why and for how long. To appeal, write to us with your Player ID within six months: someone who did not take the first decision looks at it again.
  • Age group: the game asks a year of birth once before online play and keeps only the age group. Players under 13 chat only with fixed phrases, get a generated name and cannot be found by name; players aged 13 to 17 start hidden from strangers with their online status off. The game can only make the age group stricter; parents, write to us if your child gave a wrong age.

Someone is bothering me

Tap ⋯ next to their message in Alliance or Gang chat, in the Alliance member list or on a friend card to Report or Block them. Blocking hides their chat and requests from you at once. We tell you what we decided about your report, and we never tell the other player who reported them. See the community rules. If someone is in danger, contact your local emergency services first.

Complaints about safety or our decisions

You can complain about illegal content, content harmful to children, a moderation decision (a removed message, a mute, a suspension, or a report we did not act on), the age group the game uses, or how we follow our online safety duties, including under the UK Online Safety Act. You do not need an account. Email us at the address above with the subject Online safety complaint, what happened and, if you play online, your Player ID. We confirm that we received it, someone who did not take the original decision looks at it, and we tell you the outcome. If we got it wrong, we restore the message or the account.

Delete my account

In the game: Settings → Online play → Account → Delete online account. Without the game: , with your Player ID or name and a deletion code: get one in Settings → Online play → Account while you still have the game (it works once, for 30 days). Without a code we first have to check the account is yours. Your village on the device goes when you uninstall the app or clear the site's data. Details are in the privacy policy.
