Contact us

Email:

If you play online, include your Player ID (Settings → Online play → Account) and, for a purchase, the date and the store's order number. Never send your save code or anyone's password.

My village is gone or looks wrong

  • Your village is saved on this device. If the game could not read a save, it keeps it: look for Earlier villages in Settings and tap Restore.
  • To move a village to another device, use Settings → Copy save code on the old device and Load from code on the new one. A village kept on our server comes back when you sign in on the new device with a sign-in you linked (Settings → Online play → Account → Sign-ins).
  • If the game says the village is open somewhere else, close the other tab or app: only the one opened last saves.

Purchases

  • If a purchase did not arrive, open the Store and tap Restore purchases while online. A purchase waiting for a parent's approval or the store arrives when it is approved.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple or Google: ask them through your App Store or Google Play purchase history. When a store refunds a purchase, what it gave can be taken back from a village kept on our server.
  • The web version of the game sells nothing.

Online play

  • No connection: you can keep building; if your village is kept on our server, the game keeps your actions and sends them when you are back online. Raids on real players, Alliances, friends and purchases need a connection.
  • Update needed: install the latest version from your store, or reload the web page.
  • Suspended: the account broke the community rules. Write to us with your Player ID if you think it is a mistake.

Someone is bothering me

Tap ⋯ next to their message, in the Alliance member list or on a friend card to Report or Block them. Blocking hides their chat and requests from you at once. See the community rules.

Delete my account

In the game: Settings → Online play → Account → Delete online account. Without the game: . Your village on the device goes when you uninstall the app or clear the site's data. Details are in the privacy policy.
