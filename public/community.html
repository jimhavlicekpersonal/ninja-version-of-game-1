Kagemura community rules

Kagemura: Ninja Clans · for player names, Alliance and Gang chat, friends, troop requests and online battles

Privacy policySupport

Kagemura is played by people of many ages, countries and languages. By using online play you agree to these rules. Breaking them can get messages removed, chat muted or an account suspended.

These rules are part of the .

Be decent

  • No harassment, bullying, threats or hate, in any language, spelling or code word.
  • No sexual content, and nothing that sexualises or endangers children.
  • No names that insult, impersonate another player, staff or a real person, or pretend to be official.
  • No spam, advertising, scams, or links to cheats.
  • Do not share personal details: yours or anyone else's (real name, address, phone, school, social accounts). Never ask other players for them.

Play fair

  • No cheating: no modified games, bots, scripts or tools that play for you or change battles. Our server checks battles and refuses ones it cannot reproduce.
  • Do not exploit bugs. Tell us about them instead (support).
  • Do not buy, sell or share accounts, or trade game items for real money.
  • Do not use other people's payment methods, or refund purchases you used in order to keep them.

If something is wrong

  • Block: tap ⋯ next to a message, a member or a friend card. You stop seeing their chat and requests at once, and they can no longer find you or send you friend requests.
  • Report: from the same ⋯ menu, choose a reason and, if you like, add a note. A message reported by three different players is hidden for everyone until staff decide.
  • If someone is in danger, contact your local emergency services first.

What staff do

Staff review reports and can remove messages, mute a player's chat and suspend accounts. Every staff action is logged. If you think we got it wrong, write to with your Player ID.

The chat and name filters catch common bad words only; they cannot catch everything in every language. Reports and blocks are how players help us with the rest.
