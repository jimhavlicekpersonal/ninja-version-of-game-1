In short

  • Without online play, your village stays on your device and the game sends us nothing about you (unless you turn on crash reports).
  • For online play we keep what the game needs: an account with a random Player ID, the name you choose, your village and battles, chat, friends and purchases.
  • We do not ask for your email address, real name, contacts or location in the game. There are no ads, no tracking across other apps, and we never sell your data.
  • You can delete your online account in the game or on the web at any time.

Age groups and younger players

Kagemura is a strategy game with cartoon combat, rated for teens and up in the app stores. Before you play online, the game asks your year of birth (and the month, only when the year alone does not settle it) without suggesting an answer, or uses the age range your device's app store provides. It keeps only the resulting age group (under 13, 13 to 17, or 18 or older), not your date of birth, and uses it only to set up the game for your age:

  • Under 13: no free-text chat (only a set of fixed phrases such as "Good luck!"), a generated player name instead of a chosen one, and other players cannot find you by name. You can still play, raid and join an Alliance.
  • 13 to 17: only friends of your friends can find you by name, and friends do not see when you were last online, until you change these in Settings.
  • Under 18 (both younger groups): a monthly spending limit, an extra "check with a grown-up" step before buying, and no limited-time offers that pop up on their own.

The age group is your own statement, not age verification. Once you have said you are under 18, the game cannot switch you back to adult on its own; our support team can, after checking. If you are a parent and believe your child gave a wrong age or shared personal details, write to us and we will help, including deleting the account. These protections apply in versions of the game released with them; older versions ask the age group only before a first purchase.

Playing without online play

Your village is saved on your device. The game does not need an account, and with online play off it sends nothing about you to us (unless you turn on crash reports, below). Gameplay statistics shown in Settings ("Analytics events") stay on the device, unless you are an adult playing online and choose to share them (below). If you play in a web browser, the website that delivers the game sees your IP address, as every website does.

Play statistics (off unless you say yes; adults only)

When you play online and have told us you are an adult, the game asks once whether you want to share play statistics; Settings has the same switch. It is off until you say yes, and the game never asks, or sends statistics, for players under 18 or whose age it does not know. When it is on, the game sends what you do in the game (sessions starting, tutorial steps, raids with their stars and destruction, upgrades and research started, offers shown, Jade spent on speed-ups and purchases with their product and price) under a random statistics id made for this purpose. The statistics id is not your Player ID and is never sent together with it; the game does not add your name, village, chat or sign-in token, and our server does not store the IP address with them. Saying yes also sends what this device recorded before (so we can see the first minutes of play). Turning the switch off deletes what that statistics id sent; saying yes again starts a new one. We keep shared statistics for 400 days and use them only in totals (for example, how many new players came back the next day). Players who share may receive slightly different timings for in-game offers, to compare which works better; prices, rewards and game rules are the same for everyone.

Crash reports (off unless you turn them on)

Versions of the game that include crash reporting show a "Send crash reports" switch in Settings. It is off until you turn it on. When it is on and the game hits an error, the game sends the error message, where in the game's code it happened, the game version and the platform to our error-tracking service (Sentry). The game does not attach your name, Player ID, village, chat or save, and removes web-address parameters and anything shaped like an email address from the message. An error message can still occasionally include text the game was working on when it failed. The service sees the IP address the report comes from; we set the service not to store IP addresses. Turning the switch off stops new reports at once.

Reports, moderation and notices

When you report a message or a player, our staff review it and can remove the message, mute chat or suspend the account (see the community rules). We tell the player who reported what happened, and we tell a player when we remove their message, mute them or suspend them, with the reason and how to appeal. We do not tell a reported player who reported them. Every staff action is logged so that decisions can be checked.

When you contact us

  • Support email. If you write to us, we receive your email address and what you send, and use them only to answer you and keep a record of that answer.
  • Web deletion requests. The account deletion page asks for your Player ID, the deletion code the game gives you, and an optional note. It does not ask for an email address. We keep the request and what we did about it as a record that it was handled.

What we do not do

We do not ask for your email address in the game, and we do not collect your contacts, precise location, photos or advertising id. There are no ads and no third-party tracking, and we do not sell your data or share it for advertising or cross-context behavioural advertising.

Who processes it, and where

We use service providers who process data only on our instructions: our hosting provider (server and database: ), our error-tracking service Sentry (crash reports only if you turn them on, and our server's own errors; data region: ), and Apple and Google (sign-in, purchases and notifications, under their own privacy policies). Our staff see what they need to answer support and moderate.

Some of these providers may process data outside your country, including outside the EU, UK or Brazil. Where data leaves the EU or UK for a country without an adequacy decision, we rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (and the UK addendum or International Data Transfer Agreement) or another transfer mechanism the law allows. You can ask us for a copy of the safeguards.

  • Open friend requests: 14 days.

  • In the game: Settings → Online play → Account → Delete online account.
  • On the web, if you can no longer open the game: . Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • access your data and get a copy, and receive the data you gave us in a commonly used, machine-readable form (portability);
  • correct it, or delete it (see above);
  • restrict how we use it while a question is being settled, and object to uses based on legitimate interests;
  • withdraw consent at any time (crash reports, play statistics, notifications: Settings), without affecting what happened before. Turning play statistics off also deletes what was shared.

Write to with your Player ID. We answer within one month (in Brazil, within the time the LGPD sets; in California, within 45 days) and may need to confirm the account is yours first. You can also complain to a data protection authority: in the EU the authority where you live or work, in the UK the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk), in Brazil the ANPD (gov.br/anpd). Residents of California have the rights of the CCPA/CPRA, including to know, delete, correct and not be discriminated against for using them; we do not sell or share personal information as those laws define it. Under the LGPD you can also ask for confirmation that we process your data, anonymisation, information about whom we share it with, and the consequences of refusing consent.

Purchases

Purchases are made through the App Store or Google Play. They handle your payment details; we never see them. The terms of service describe Jade and other purchases.

Security

The game talks to our server only over encrypted connections (HTTPS), sign-in tokens are stored as one-way hashes, and staff access is limited and logged. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Changes

We update this page and its date when what we collect changes, and tell players in the game about important changes.
