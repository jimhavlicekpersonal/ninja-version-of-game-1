SupportCommunity rules

Kagemura ("the game") is published by ("we"). Questions about privacy or your data: .

Playing without online play

Your village is saved on your device. The game does not need an account, and with online play off it sends nothing about you to us (unless you turn on crash reports, below). Gameplay statistics shown in Settings ("Analytics events") stay on the device and are never sent. If you play in a web browser, the website that delivers the game sees your IP address, as every website does.

What we collect for online play, and why

  • Your online account. When online play is on, the game creates an account: a random Player ID, a secret sign-in token (we store only a one-way hash of it), the player name you choose, a friend code, your privacy choices (who can find you by name, whether friends see when you were last online) and when you were last online. Other players see your name.
  • Your game. Your base for raids and, once your village is kept on our server, the village itself and the actions you take in it; battles (replays are kept 30 days), honor, rankings and ratings, and a history of resources added or spent. We use them so online play works, raids are fair and checked, and you can continue on another device.
  • Alliances, friends and Gangs. Chat messages, troop requests and troops you send, your friends list and friend requests (open requests lapse after 14 days), Gangs, Gang Wars and Friendly Duels.
  • Reports and blocks. When you report a message or a player we keep the report, and the reported text until we have looked at it and for 90 days after our decision. Blocks are kept until you unblock.
  • Sign-ins you link (optional). If you link Sign in with Apple, Google, Game Center or Play Games, we keep only that service's id for you, never your email address or name. If you ask the game to find your Game Center or Play Games friends, we use their ids to see which of them play Kagemura; we do not keep that list.
  • Purchases. The store's transaction id, the product, the price and whether it was refunded, to deliver each purchase once and handle refunds. The age group you give before a first purchase (under 18 or not) keeps players under 18 within a monthly spending limit.
  • Notifications (optional). If you turn on notifications: your device's notification token, the types you chose and your time-zone offset, so we do not notify you at night or too often.
  • Network data. Our server sees your IP address with each request. We use it to stop abuse (rate limits) and keep it only in short-lived counters and in server logs.
  • Server error reports. When our server itself fails, it may send the error, the route and the request method and path (without query strings or request contents) to our error-tracking service.

Crash reports (off unless you turn them on)

Versions of the game that include crash reporting show a "Send crash reports" switch in Settings. It is off until you turn it on. When it is on and the game hits an error, the game sends the error message, where in the game's code it happened, the game version and the platform to our error-tracking service (Sentry). The game does not attach your name, Player ID, village, chat or save, and removes web-address parameters and anything shaped like an email address from the message. An error message can still occasionally include text the game was working on when it failed. The service sees the IP address the report comes from.

When you contact us

  • Support email. If you write to us, we receive your email address and what you send, and use them only to answer you and keep a record of that answer.
  • Web deletion requests. The account deletion page asks for your Player ID or player name and an optional note. It does not ask for an email address. We keep the request and what we did about it as a record that it was handled.

What we do not do

We do not ask for your email address in the game, and we do not collect your contacts, precise location, photos or advertising id. There are no ads and no third-party tracking, and we do not sell your data or share it for advertising.

Who processes it

Our hosting provider (server and database), our error-tracking service (Sentry: crash reports only if you turn them on, and our server's own errors), and Apple and Google (sign-in, purchases, notifications). They process data for us.

How long we keep it

As long as your online account exists, except: battle replays 30 days; open friend requests 14 days; handled reports 90 days after the decision; server logs and rate-limit counters as short as our host allows. Purchase records are kept after an account is deleted, without your Player ID, because tax and refund rules require them.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your online account in the game (Settings → Online play → Account → Delete online account) or on the web: . This removes your online account, name, base, chat messages, friends, linked sign-ins and the village kept on our server. Raids you made stay in other players' replays as "Deleted player".
  • Your village on the device is removed by uninstalling the app or clearing the site's data in your browser; Settings → "Start a new village" replaces it.
  • Turn notifications, crash reports and online play off at any time in Settings.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, or to correct or delete it: . Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California) you have these rights by law and can complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Kagemura is not directed at children under 13. Players who say they are under 18 have a monthly spending limit and are reminded to ask a parent before buying.

Changes

We update this page and its date when what we collect changes, and tell players in the game about important changes.
