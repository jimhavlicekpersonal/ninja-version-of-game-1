# Decisions (the detail behind the root brief)

Moved out of the root `CLAUDE.md` in the state-of-the-game report, section 5 step 10 (quality 11), so the brief every session loads stays short. Current version numbers are in `docs/STATUS.md` (generated); the text below may name older ones as history.

## Decisions

- **Identity:** display names come from `src/gameplay/data/*.json`. Internal ids are unchanged (`docs/rebuild/ID_MAP.md`). The app display name is "Kagemura". The bundle id is still the old one until new store listings exist (owner step); store product ids have their own names (`store.json` `storeProductIds`: `jade_pouch` … `season_scroll`), mapped from the game's unchanged ids.
- **Art:** all art is still generated in code (`src/presentation/art/ninja.ts`), with a look per level (re-audit: the Keep has 6 visual tiers and defenses 5, and every level inside a tier still adds a visible detail; tested in `tests/presentation/art/levels-and-sprites.test.ts`), roof materials by family (thatch, kawara tile, copper) and an original kamon set. Each of the 50 warriors has a role silhouette matching its badge, with a role ring in battle. Each building shows its job: resource by colour and pictogram (gold coin = coins, straw sheaf = rice), producers look busy (chimney smoke, furnace glow, water), stores look shut (namako lattice, locks, stacked bales), and noren curtains or signboards carry a mark (crossed blades = Dojo, hammer = Artisan's Hut, crest = Lodge). Resolution: two profiles (`render/quality.ts`): `high` (canvas up to 3x, buildings 2.5x, warriors/badges 3x, island 1.5x) and `low` (2x, 30 FPS); building levels are painted on first use, so a new village holds ~62 MB of textures on high and ~26 MB on low (measured in emulation; real memory is several times the texture figure, see the audit's re-audit record). Painted building art is capped per profile (`render/textureBudget.ts`, `paintedBudgetMB` 64 high / 24 low): scenes declare what they show (`holdBuildingTextures`), and the least recently used rest is removed and repainted when needed (report section 5 step 3). Mountains, fog, village life, ruins, hit flashes and tracers live in `render/fx.ts`; sound is koto/shamisen plucks and taiko (`audio/sound.ts`), haptics `audio/haptics.ts`. Fonts: M PLUS 1 for text and Dela Gothic One for titles and big labels (`--font-display`, `DISPLAY_FONT`; one weight, always 400), self-hosted in `src/presentation/ui/fonts/` (OFL). The plateau floats on a sea of clouds (`ground.ts`, `addSurroundings` in `render/assets.ts`). HUD icons are gradient SVGs whose ids `icon()` makes unique per copy. App icon and splash screens come from `npm run brand` (`tools/brand/brand.mjs`; needs Playwright). Real images override any asset key through `public/art/manifest.json`. `npm run art` shows a gallery. ART AI brief: `docs/art/ART_AI_BRIEF.md`.
- **Save format** is versioned (`src/gameplay/sim/save.ts`, now **v21**: buildings may carry `temper` and raid records `stake`/`carry`, and a temper the rules do not allow is dropped on every load (balance audit 2026-11-08); v20: purchase records keep what a Clan Stipend added (`stipend`), what a Season Scroll's premium claims gave (`premium`) and what a refund took (`refund`), so refunds take those back and reversed refunds give back exactly that (remnant audit 2026-11-06); v19: the 28-day calendar and the device's UTC offset for local task days; v18: a Clan Stipend keeps the Jade it was sold with (`stipend.jade`) and chests note the streak's own Jade; v17: refund debts, any resource may be negative; v16: Clan Stipend and goal events, purchase grants on records, new defense stats and adaptive task targets; v15: `profile.ageBand` (age bands); v14: `flags.sandbox` marks testing-sandbox villages; v13: Gang War chests collected; v12: donated-troop levels, research lanes, army presets, settled-season log, tasks fixed per period), with a migration step per version. Bump `SAVE_VERSION` for every state change. `repairTrainingQueue` runs on every load: one bad timer must never cost a player their village.
- **Save versions for features that are not live (lesson from v23, third verification fixes 2026-11-14):** v23 was bumped only to add two empty fields (`mastery`, `hero`) for content that ships switched off, and it forces "Update Kagemura" on every older build that adopts a server-held village written by a v23 server (a save from a newer build stops the boot). The version is never moved back. The invariant stands: a change to what a village stores is a state change and gets a bump and a migration step. What changes is how such work is planned: (1) a field for a not-live feature is added only when the feature that writes it is about to go live, not when its code is merged; until then the code reads it as optional with a default (`state.hero ?? {}`) and nothing writes it, so the stored format does not change and no bump is due; (2) when a bump is due anyway, group the not-live fields into it rather than bumping for them alone; (3) every bump that reaches server-held villages is released with the server and the game together and the forced-update notice planned (`docs/SHIPPING.md` 3g "Save format releases"). A field that is written (even empty) by `createNewVillage` or a migration is a state change: it needs the bump.
- **Friend-invite rewards are for adults only (third verification fixes 2026-11-14, conservative default the owner can revisit):** an under-18 or unstated age band neither enters a friend code (the entry is a no-op with a kind line: "Friend invite rewards are for adults only. You can still add friends and play together."), nor collects, nor makes the inviter collect; a 13–17 inviter's code is refused like any other refusal (no age is revealed). Reasons: a Jade reward for recruiting friends is an incentive aimed at a child's social circle (COPPA's and the UK Children's Code's concern with nudges and data from children; Apple 5.1.4, Google's Families policy), the age band is self-declared, and minors already get no offers that pop up. Changing it is a code change in `server/core/referrals.ts` (`adult`) and the invite panel (`ReferralView.adultsOnly`); a legal review per market should come first (`docs/legal/MARKETS.md`).
- **One stable installation id per game install (third verification fixes 2026-11-14):** `tk.device.install` (`Connection.installId`), made once and kept in device storage, is sent with a friend code, a reward claim and purchase intents so "no shared device" and refund counts see the same phone across villages and accounts (the per-village queue id stays for command numbering). It is random, never the statistics id, stored server-side only with the account (`player_installs`, deleted with it). Clearing the browser's storage makes a new one; that is accepted. The public privacy page names it (last gaps 2026-11-14), with purchase intents and purchases keeping it as their device.
- **Under-13 players in `UNDER13_MODE=offline` (last gaps 2026-11-14):** they are not online at all, so they are left out of both leaderboards (honor and Power), matchmaking and memberships. In `limited`/`families` they stay on the leaderboards under their generated name: a board row carries no player id, and a friend request by name is refused for an under-13 player in every mode, so a listing is no way to find or contact them. Friend suggestions are names the game already showed (raiders, Alliance mates) and go through the same refusal.
- **Shared replays and the defender's base (third verification fixes 2026-11-14):** a real defender is never named, and when the server finds the raid (the sharer's attack record with that seed) the defender's base is shared only if the defender is an adult who has not turned off "Allow my base in other players' shared replays" (default on for adults, never for minors or unstated bands); turning it off, a stricter band and account deletion remove the shares of that base. Last gaps 2026-11-14 (Honesty): a replay the server has no record of (older than its 30-day attack records, a forged seed, a duel) is never labelled a real player's; a request saying `player` is stored as `practice` ("A practice battle.", the label that claims no real opponent), and `generated`/`practice` requests and the base markers stand. A raid on the sharer's own base that the server recorded (a shared defense) counts as a record. Refusing such shares was the alternative; it was not chosen because it would stop players sharing their own older battles while the label already promises nothing untrue.
- **One writer per village:** every stored save carries a revision and `saveStore.save` refuses to write over one it didn't read (`SaveConflictError`), so a tab left open can't erase newer progress. The tab opened last owns the village; the other shows "Open somewhere else". A save the game can't read is kept (`backupDamaged`) and offered back at start-up and in Settings (`controller.backups`, `restoreBackup`). A save from a newer build stops the boot instead of being replaced.
- **Battle rules** are versioned (`SIM_RULES` in `sim/battle/battle.ts`, now 7: a Watch-tempered base also cuts the attackers' support (`temper.troopCuts`), so rules 6 is archived in `server/rules/v6` and is not compatible; 6: Tempering (`BaseBuilding.temper`, `sim/battle/temper.ts`), and an untempered base plays exactly as under 5, so `SIM_RULES_COMPATIBLE` is [5, 4] and rules 5 is archived in `server/rules/v5`; 5: heroes (`BattleSetup.hero`, the Shadow Captain from Keep 7, report section 5 step 8); `SIM_RULES_COMPATIBLE` is [4] because a battle without a hero plays exactly as under 4; rules 4 (the design re-audit's role budgets and Shadow stealth) and rules 3 (relay strikes via `BattleSetup.startDamage`) are archived in `server/rules/v4` and `v3` so older games still play online; rules 2 is retired). Records carry the rules they were fought under; older replays are shown as unavailable, never replayed wrongly. `tests/gameplay/battle/rules-version.test.ts` fails when combat data changes without a bump.
- **Protocol** is v11 (balance audit 2026-11-08: features `tempering` and `stakes`; `POST /v1/attacks/start` carries the declared stake, the ticket keeps it, `AttackResponse.stake`; tempered bases validated on upload; migration 0030 `tickets.stake`; all additions, servers still accept 10, and 7 except `match`). v10: Ledger audit (`docs/audit/STATE_OF_THE_GAME.md` section 5 step 1, recorded in `docs/store/WORLDWIDE_AUDIT.md`): the battle seed comes from `POST /v1/attacks/start`, a started raid with no report is a loss at the next match after 8 minutes, a server-held village rerolls opponents only through a paid Next, raid reports lock both players, the under-18 cap is re-checked at redeem, imports above Keep 5 need account age, refunds leave debts in every resource, and passive war chests were cut. Report section 5 step 7 adds `rules-acceptance` (`PUT /v1/me/rules`: chat, Alliance/Gang names and renames need the current community rules accepted on the account, `403 rules_required`), App Store `RESCIND_CONSENT` (online play suspended for that account), friend requests by id under the search rules, names refusing contact handles, and deletion that purges stored answers and holds the name 35 days. Re-audit additions are additive and announced in `/v1/hello` features: `attack-commit` (`POST /v1/attacks/start|void`: a started raid that is never reported settles as a loss; one open raid per attacker; a defender shielded by someone else meanwhile makes a late report practice, `409 defender_protected`), purchase-intent release, age bands (`PUT /v1/me/age`), deletion codes, notices, Sign in with Apple webhooks, per-device command numbers (`CommandBatch.device`; a batch without one is refused once a village has device numbers), gzip/br responses. Friendly Duels, Gangs, Gang Wars, relay strikes (rules 3), `503 busy` from the battle-checking queue. v8: game versions (`X-Data-Version`, tickets checked with the rules they were issued under, `supportedRules`/`minClientBuild` in hello, "Update Kagemura"), balance bundles, unique names and friends (`docs/PROTOCOL.md`). v7: the server owns the economy; every request carries `X-Protocol`, `X-Sim-Rules`, `X-Client-Build`, writes an `Idempotency-Key`; older games get `426 version_mismatch` and are told to update). v6 brought the audit fixes (donated levels, idempotent tickets, server-confirmed loot, legal bases and armies, equal war rosters).
- **Server (roadmap code steps 10–48 done; protocol 7 from step 26; `docs/backend/PVP_ROADMAP.md` marks each step, owner steps stay unticked with what was prepared):**
  - *Hardening (Phase 1):* the server issues each ticket's battle seed and refuses reports played with another; each base is stored once by content hash and replays are kept 30 days; database changes are numbered migrations run by `npm run server:migrate` as a release step (the server won't boot with pending ones); rate limits per IP, token and costly route (`429 rate_limited`, retryable for the client) and body limits; `DELETE /v1/me`, a web deletion page (`/account/delete`) and Settings → Account; reports, blocks, auto-hide after 3 reporters, chat mutes and a staff queue behind `ADMIN_TOKEN`.
  - *Scale and operations:* optional Redis (`REDIS_URL`) for the leaderboard sorted set, match locks (two attackers never get the same defender) and shared rate limits, through a small hand-written RESP client; JSON logs with request ids, Sentry envelopes without the SDK (server `SENTRY_DSN`, game `VITE_SENTRY_DSN` + Settings switch), `/healthz` and `/readyz`, Prometheus metrics behind `METRICS_TOKEN` (re-sim time, battles, desyncs from `BattleSubmission.clientHash`, errors by code). `npm run server:bench` measured re-simulation at 5 ms (Keep 1) to ~0.45 s p95 (Keeps 8–15) on a 2.1 GHz vCPU: a worker pool (roadmap step 57) is needed before real load.
  - *Server-owned village (Phase 2, steps 19–26):* the game moves its village to the server when it connects (`POST /v1/village/import`, refused if its Keep could not hold it; Settings says why), or starts a new one there. The game queues each economy command (`PLAYER_COMMANDS`, `sim/serverEconomy.ts`) with its time and `economyHash`; the server replays it with the same `applyCommand`, clamps times to its clock, and sends corrections when the hashes differ (the server wins). Attacks, war attacks, chests and gifts settle server-held villages in one transaction with the result, with an append-only ledger. Linked sign-ins (Apple, Google, Game Center, Play Games; `server/identity/verify.ts`) restore the account and its village on another device (cloud save). Purchases stay trusted from the device until Phase 4.
  - *Ranking (Phase 3, steps 27–32):* hidden Attack and Base Ratings (`sim/rating.ts`, `ranks.json` `rating`) pick the fairest base after the Keep window and power floor; bots never move a rating; a defender loses at most 150 honor a UTC day; a rebuilt layout restarts its Base Rating; a Power Ranking (skill plus Keep) beside the Honor leaderboard. Convergence is simulated in `tests/gameplay/balance/rating-convergence.test.ts`.
  - *Payments (Phase 4, steps 35–38):* the server checks every store receipt before delivery (`server/payments/apple.ts`: StoreKit 2 JWS against Apple's pinned root; `google.ts`: Play Developer API, consume or acknowledge, RTDN and Voided Purchases), credits each store transaction once, applies the under-18 cap across devices before the store is asked (purchase intents), and takes refunds back from a server-held village (`revokePurchase`; a reversed refund gives back what it took, `reinstatePurchase`). The game finishes a store transaction only after delivery; unfinished ones are delivered later; Store → Restore purchases. Remnant audit (2026-11-06): Google purchases are consumed by the server only when its own village holds them (acknowledged otherwise, so a lost answer or failed save on the device is delivered again); a receipt the server knows was refunded is refused; a credited purchase sent again after its village moved to the server is delivered there; and the game applies the server's refund records to its own village at start and on reconnection (`GameController.settleRefunds`; for a server-held village only the stipend, Scroll and looks, since the server owns the resources). Android purchases only in builds made with `VITE_PLAY_BILLING=1`.
  - *Push and deployments (steps 40, 42, 43):* server push through APNs/FCM (raided, donations, Alliance War found; per-device types, quiet hours, `maxPerDay`); `DEPLOY_ENV` (production refuses to start without its essentials; a database belongs to one deployment); `.github/workflows/server.yml` (tests → Docker image → staging → contract tests, production by hand).
  - *Operations (steps 44–48):* encrypted daily backups with a weekly automated restore drill (`tools/ops/`, `.github/workflows/backup.yml`), two-instance test, IPv6 listening and HSTS (`tools/ops/check-domain.mjs`), `npm run server:load` (one instance holds ~3 full-army battles/s: re-simulation blocks the event loop, so the worker pool, step 57, comes before launch), alert rules (`ops/prometheus/alerts.yml`) and `SERVER_NOTICE`, the staff tool at `/admin` (lookup, give/take, suspend, rating reset, staff log).
  - *Store submission kit (owner steps 49–53):* `docs/store/` and `npm run store:screenshots`.
  - Details: `docs/PROTOCOL.md`, `docs/SHIPPING.md`, `docs/backend/PVP_ROADMAP.md`.
- **Infiltration Points** (`src/gameplay/sim/battle/infiltration.ts`, `infiltration` in `battle.json`): warriors enter only inside the base's 6 points (map corners and edge midpoints, an opposite pair closed by the layout), and each point hides a third of the army's housing (minimum 15), so a full army comes in as at least three squads. Smoke Bombs still go anywhere. Scouting shows the points and the defenses covering each path to the Keep. Battles without `setup.infiltration` (records from before the rule) keep free placement. With it, a planned attack (mixed squads, chosen approach) beats a lazy one by about 9–29 points (Keeps 4–8) and best vs worst approach differ by 15–40 (`tests/gameplay/balance/placement.test.ts`). Defense damage ×1.3 and HP ×1.15 at every level, so a planned attack lands at ~60–80% and brute force falls short.
- **Ninja tactics:** Smoke Bomb (warriors *inside* the cloud can only be shot from defense range × 0.4, never below 2 tiles; 8 s, 1 + 1 per 40 housing charges, max 3, no stacking). It is taught as "smoke your warriors as they walk into tower range", which is what the rule rewards. Sabotage (Saboteur jams a defense 6 s; it is then immune for 10 s after recovering; one jam each). All numbers are in `battle.json` and `troops.json`.
- **Live defensive counters** (`src/gameplay/sim/battle/fortifications.ts`, `effect` in `buildings.json`): Paper Lantern Line (Keep 5, lights stealth/veiled warriors), Clockwork Repair Crane and Bitter Smoke Brazier (Keep 6, shorten jams, stuns and Hawk Tamer slowing / cut healing and auras), Kite-Cutter Ballista (Keep 8, a control tower: its bolt grounds a flyer 3 s, then 3 s immunity, and ground defenses finish it), Longbow Yagura (Keep 12, range 13, prefers attackers with range ≥ 3.5, blind inside 2 tiles). Soft: no stacking, short disables. A building being upgraded neither fires nor runs its effect, and scouting doesn't count it. Generated bases put the Crane and Incense next to the defenses they cover. Tests: `tests/gameplay/battle/counters.test.ts`, `audit-ai.test.ts`.
- **Late-Keep curve:** every building level first unlocked after Keep 6 has HP and damage × 1.56 × 1.15^(Keep − 6), because armies outgrew bases (every army scored 100% from Keep 9). On top, defense levels get a measured mid-Keep bump (Keep 5 ×1.08, 6 ×1.15, 7–8 ×1.2 tapering to 12 ×1.04, 13–15 ×1.06; `tools/balance/midkeep.mjs`). Keep 7 was a hole (its bases fielded Keep 6 levels while armies gained two research levels and a new tier; every army scored ~100%): Keep 7 now unlocks Kunai Tower level 9, Shuriken Tower level 8 and Firework Mortar level 6, and the two towers' Keep 6 levels are ×1.5 so the step into the late curve is no longer ×2.17. Measure with `npm run balance:armies` → `docs/game-theory/ARMY_BALANCE.md`; outcome gates at Keeps 7, 8 and 15 in `tests/gameplay/balance/army-lab.test.ts` (no army over 92% or 60% three-stars on same-Keep bases; ranged-heavy within 20 points of a balanced mix).
- **Air pays for flying:** the Sky role's power budget is lower (efficiency 0.494, HP/DPS 2.7: air troops have 0.45× the HP and 0.75× the damage of the old numbers), because flyers skip walls and every ground-only defense; air used to win ~100% at every Keep. Anti-air keeps up late: Sky Bow Tower goes to level 10 with a third tower from Keep 11, and Sky Bow and Ballista HP is ×1.6.
- **Range pays for safety:** Throwers deal 0.7× damage (efficiency 0.627, HP/DPS 3.43), because they fire from behind the front line and over walls (ranged mixes three-starred 69–100%). Warriors that still measured too strong carry a DPS tax (`dpsScale` in `roster.json`): Siege Bombardier 0.5 (and range 5, inside a Cannon's reach), Thousand Needle 0.6 (range 5), Hawk Tamer 0.6. See `docs/game-theory/PLAYABLE_ROSTER.md`.
- **Matchmaking follows earned power:** opponents are never weaker than the attacker's own strength (Keep and research progress on the Keep's level scale, where a newer tier's level 1 counts as 1 + `levelCapOffset`; `attackerStrength`) minus `powerSlackByKeep` (0.75 at Keeps 1-4, where a just-arrived army met near-capped bases of the Keep below and Keep 3 averaged 0.88 stars, and 1.0 at Keep 3; since the balance audit 2026-11-08, where strength counts only the fielded army, a lost defence no longer lowers honor and outposts are tempered: 0.25 at Keep 5, 0.5 at 6, 0.15 at 7, 0.75 at the measured dips of Keeps 8, 12 and 13 and 0.5 elsewhere, set against the campaign gates and the matched-raid farm bound; Keep 8 at 1.0 made matched raids a three-star farm and Keep 7 at 0 meets Keep 8 bases), so a maxed Keep 15 army meets Keep 15 bases even while its honor lags (the audit measured 97% three-star raids at Keep 15). `npm run balance:campaign` → `docs/game-theory/CAMPAIGN.md`.
- **Buying resources with Jade** is a top-up, never an open exchange: `{ type: 'topUp', then: action }` buys exactly what one upgrade, building, Fill or research is missing and starts it in the same step (nothing is charged if it can't start). The price is Keep-scaled (`topUpScaleByHall` in `store.json`, from `tools/balance/topup.mjs`): a raid's worth of loot costs ~70 Jade at Keep 1 and ~2,400 at Keep 15, where the old flat curve swung 1,700×. Bought resources never count as collected. Offered on building sheets, Build menu cards (bought when the spot is confirmed) and in the Army panel (Train: `Buy & Fill N`; Research: `Buy missing`). Finishing army training is priced on the same Keep scale (`trainingSkipScaleByHall`, never below the plain time price) so skip-and-raid stays within 10× of a top-up for the same loot (it was 65× at Keep 15). Report section 5 step 6 (economy E01–E05, E19): building and research skips cost ~40% less (`skipCurve` 1 h 15, 1 day 180, 1 week 720) while finishing training keeps the old curve (`trainingSkipCurve`); the Clan Stipend pays 12 Jade a payment (360 for $2.99, under the $9.99 Box); the streak's top-step Jade comes once a week (`jadeEveryDays`); three Keep looks unlock at Keeps 5, 8 and 11 (`minKeep`); the Keep Celebration offer (`offer_keep`) can follow a Keep level; the Starter Pack is re-armed until it shows and comes before the Artisan's Bundle (`afterOffer`); the age is asked once when the tutorial ends. Analysis: `docs/game-theory/TOP_UP.md`; tests: `tests/gameplay/economy/topup.test.ts`.
- **Return hooks and a face** (report section 5 step 8): a next-objective chip (`sim/objective.ts`); notifications asked once after the first long timer (still off until yes; the daily-chest reminder on after opt-in); task days at local midnight (`sim/calendar.ts` `localDayIndex`, `setLocalTime` at most weekly; chest and stipend keep UTC); a fixed 28-day calendar (`daily.json` `calendar`, a Keep look on day 28); generated opponents are outposts of three named computer rival clans (`names.json` `rivalClans`, `sim/rivals.ts`); a new village starts with decor around the Keep (`economy.json` `startingDecor`); the Shadow Captain hero (`battle.json` `heroes`, `sim/heroes.ts`).
- **Tempering** (balance audit 2026-11-08; `sim/battle/temper.ts`, `battle.json` `temper`; design `docs/game-theory/DESIGN_TEMPERING_2026-11-08.md`, calibration `docs/game-theory/TEMPERING_CALIBRATION_2026-11-09.md`): from Keep 5 the owner shapes every Kunai Tower and every Shuriken Tower (per type) along Reach (+1 range, from 5 points prefers attackers fighting from 3.5+ tiles, −40% damage at full), Blast (Kunai only: 0.35-tile splash, fire interval ×1.8, −30% damage) and Watch (−10% damage; base-wide by the base's mean temper: Sky Bow +30% damage and 40% faster, Bitter Smoke Brazier +2 radius and +50% cuts, Spike Pits and Sap Puddles ×2), in whole points, 10 in total. One `temper` command per type: 5% of the type's cumulative Coin cost, no builder, no timer; a new tower takes its type's temper. Calibrated on the real simulation so no pure profile is a dominant defence against the raid bots' army at Keeps 6–15; at Keep 15 no army is best against every profile any more (Blast stops Eclipse Ninjas 97% → 60%, Watch stops air 91% → 34%, air answers Blast), and since battle rules 7 (2026-11-11) not at Keep 12 either: Watch also cuts the attackers' healing, auras, the Mountain Shield's splash cover and revives across the base (`temper.troopCuts`), which takes the searched mixed army from 95% to 70% against a Watch base (the next army 70.4%) while Watch stays neutral against the raid bots' army. Scouting always shows the profile and its leaning. Rival clans lean on one axis each (Crimson Lotus Reach, Iron Tide Blast with ×1.1 loot and buildings 0.1 closer to their caps, Moon Veil Watch with ×0.9 and 0.1 further), and with `offers.counterShare` (50%, seeded) an outpost counters the family of the player's last ten raid armies, so Next is a choice. The design's Sky re-budget was measured unnecessary and dropped; a far-target damage bonus for Reach was tried and removed.
- **Raids and honor after the balance audit (round 2, `docs/game-theory/SIMULATION_ROUND2_2026-11-09.md`):** staked honor (`ranks.json`: `defenseHonorLossShare` 0, a lost defence costs no honor; `attackWin.perTrophyBelow` 0.25, a win against a base with less honor pays less, down to nothing, so honor counts how well a player raids and holds, not how often they were farmed); exhausted stores (`raids.exhaustion`: each raid a village lost in the last 24 h halves the next raider's loot, offline and online); exposure targeting (online, a village raided within `exposureHours` 12 is offered after every one that was not); an upward loot penalty (`lootPenaltyUpByHallDiff` 0.9 / 0.8, lagging a Keep behind no longer pays); diversity carries more (`raids.diversity`: a raid carries 0.85–1.0 of its loot by the Gini-Simpson diversity of the roles that fought; `sim/carry.ts`); opt-in declared stakes (`raids.stakes.byStars`: declare 2★ for ×1.1 or 3★ for ×1.25 before the raid; a miss carries nothing and a real defender keeps the loot; online the stake is fixed at the raid's start, before the seed); walls and traps payable in Provisions one for one (`economy.provisionsPayable`, `pay: 'dew'`).

## Engagement features (and their limits)

Built to bring players back for good reasons, within App Store rules, FTC guidance and loot-box laws. Keep these rules when changing them:

- **Daily chest** (`src/gameplay/sim/daily.ts`, `src/gameplay/data/daily.json`): free, one per UTC day, contents drawn from a weighted table whose exact odds are shown before opening. The draw comes from the village seed + day, so reloading can't reroll it. It is never sold: no Moonstone or real-money way to open, reopen or reroll it. If a paid random item is ever added, its odds must be shown before purchase (App Review Guideline 3.1.1) and the loot-box laws in each target market checked first.
- **Streak**: one step per day on a 7-step ladder of bigger chests (the top step's own Jade is paid once a week, report section 5 step 6). A missed day never wipes it: a free weekly skip covers one day, otherwise you move back one step per missed day. Skips and streak repairs are never sold.
- **Season Scroll after the last tier and at the rollover** (2026-11-18, P1-01, P1-03): tasks keep paying a Keep-scaled resource bonus past the last tier (`pass.json` `bonus`, at most 17 a season, never Jade, kept out of the value estimate), and a season that ends grants every unlocked unclaimed tier, premium ones while owned (recorded on the purchase for refunds), with what did not fit reported. Limits kept: nothing taken for a missed day, nothing sold, no countdown added. The paid look changes each season (`seasonSkins`); a buyer who owns it gets 60 Jade instead of nothing.
- **Limited-time events** (`src/gameplay/sim/events.ts`, `src/gameplay/data/events.json`): a fixed schedule; since 2026-11-18 (P5-01) a 28-day loop of thirteen windows (ten names) with quiet days between, plus five festivals on fixed UTC dates (`dated`, key `<id>#<year>`). Start and end times come only from the schedule; nothing resets or extends them, so every countdown is real. Offers keep their deadline too (tested). Over any year a player meets 15 names, no two weeks are alike, and at most half of all hours have an event (`events-loop.test.ts`). Festival names are the game's own (Clan New Year, Blossom Wind, Midsummer Lanterns, Moon Viewing, First Snow), dated away from religious observances; they are on the owner's cultural-review list.
- **Festival looks and goal progress per occurrence** (2026-11-18, P5-01): each festival is a goal event whose three-star step gives its look (`cosmetics.json` `earned`, never sold, granted once). Goal progress is kept per occurrence (`VillageState.goalEvents`, save v24, at most 8), so two goal events running at once (the Gauntlet beside a Bounty) never reset each other's ladder.
- **The daily contract** (2026-11-18, P5-03): one a UTC day, the same for everyone (`raids.json` `contracts`, `list[day mod 6]`, `sim/carry.ts` `contractFor`): a rival clan's outposts carry 20% more, a reached stake 15% more, the army's mix counts double, or three-star raids 15% more. It changes only what a won raid carries home, never the battle, needs no stored state, and is computed from the raid's own time on device and server. Everything that multiplies a raid's own loot (contract, stake, diversity, event bonus) stops at `raids.carryCap` 2.2x the plain loot; the event bonus gives way (`cappedCarry`, tested over two years of events).
- **Tasks: 23 templates** (2026-11-18, P5-04): eight more (a new Trial tier, donations, the daily drill, a reached stake, a three-star raid; weekly a rematch, goal-event ladder steps, three-star raids) on three new counters (`raidsThreeStar`, `stakesWon`, `goalSteps`, filled by `repairStats` within save v24). Each is offered only when the village can finish it (`taskEligible`: an Alliance, a camp, recent raid rates, a raid still answerable, a goal event in the week). A collect task's target is above what the largest collector holds, so no task is finished by one collect. Measured (`scripts/retention/r1.mjs`): the same three daily tasks as the day before on 0.3% of days, two of three on 10.8% (was 28%).
- **Achievements: 21** (2026-11-18, P5-05): twelve more (donations, rematches, warriors trained, destruction; calendar days, warriors unlocked, looks owned, tower kinds tempered read from the village; three-star raids, stakes reached, Gauntlet ladders completed (new counter `gauntletLadders`); raids held, added because the fortress campaign holds 42.7% at Keeps 9-15, over the 40% the item asked). Every tier is reachable (`achievements-reachable.test.ts`), each has its platform ids (`achievementIds.json`; Play Games ids are the owner's to paste). **Free Jade:** the twelve add 575 Jade once in all (one-time free Jade from achievements was 2,540, now 3,115); no monthly free Jade changes.
- **Earned looks** (2026-11-18, P5-06): fifteen two-colour looks granted in play and never sold (`cosmetics.json` `earned`): the first season finished in Obsidian, Storm and Kage (`ranks.json` leagues `look`, in `settleSeasons`); Trial tiers 20, 40 and 55 (`trials.json` `looks`; the item asked for 60, but tier 58 is the measured ceiling of the raid bots' mix); calendar cycles 2 to 6 (`daily.json` `calendar.skins`: that cycle's day 28 gives the look instead of 40 Jade, so free Jade falls by 200 over cycles 2-6); lifetime chests opened 30, 100, 200 and 365 (new counter `chestsOpened`, which a missed day cannot touch). The Looks tab says "Looks collected: N of M".
- **Warrior Mastery as ranks** (2026-11-18, P5-07): live (`features.mastery` in the shipped data) with no stat bonus (`progression.json` `mastery.maxBonus` 0, `xpBase` 1500: rank 5 near 40 raids for a warrior that is a quarter of a Keep 12 army, about rank 10 after a month, under 40 after a year). No battle setup carries mastery, so no battle changes. Experience is paid for raids and a Trial tier's first clear only (a replayed tier farmed it); Alliance War, Gang War, Bounty, Gauntlet and rematch battles pay none, and the result screen says what a raid or first clear paid. **Open, with what it needs:** the stat bonus would need a retuned curve with a cap near +15%, a place in `attackerStrength` or a limit to generated bases and Trials, a mastered campaign policy with a gate, the server's truth of BNL-06, the curve inside `GameData` so it is versioned, and a rules bump because archived engines ignore the field.
- **Keep seals** (2026-11-18, P5-08): when every building a Keep allows is at that Keep's cap (`hall.json` limits), the Keep sheet offers its seal: 30,000 Coins and Provisions at the Keep 5 economy, Keep-scaled, once per Keep (`hall.json` `seal`, `claimKeepSeal`, `VillageState.seals`). Fifteen medium-horizon goals from existing data and a quiet pull against hollow Keep rushing; nothing is forced. The honor panel lists the sealed Keeps.
- **One daily clock** (2026-11-18, P5-09): the free chest, the streak's skip week and the Clan Stipend roll at the player's midnight (`localDayIndex`), like tasks and the calendar. The switch is lazy: the first claim after the update still follows the old UTC day and then stores the local day (`daily.local`, `stipend.local`, save v24 optional), so the switch never allows a second chest on the day it happens and never takes one away. `LOCAL_TIME_CHANGE_DAYS` already limits clock moves. The refusal no longer says "(UTC)".
- **Season identity** (2026-11-18, P5-10): every Scroll season and monthly league season is named from a list of twelve (`pass.json` `seasonNames`, season number mod 12), shown on the Scroll and league panels. Every Scroll rollover writes a recap (`pass.lastSeason`: tiers reached, the league at the rollover, raids won and stars in the season from `pass.startStats`), shown once on the welcome-back card with a Share button the player presses (the device's share sheet, plain text, no name).
- **Honest Jade sinks** (2026-11-18, P5-11): six more Keep looks for Jade at the existing price ladder (450 at Keeps 6 and 7, 650 at 9 and 10, 900 at 12 and 14; 13 looks for Jade, 3,900 Jade more to own them all). Two colours and a name each: no power and no product (tested). Left out, recorded as open ideas: raising `queuedArmies` to 2 (unmeasured effect on pace), a raider-clan forecast in the Events panel (wait for P2-03 to prove itself), real-player revenge online (server and protocol work with moderation consequences).
- **The weekly Gauntlet** (2026-11-18, P5-02): every weekend, one generated fortress per Keep, seeded by the occurrence key and the Keep only, so "everyone at your Keep fights this base this week" is literally true (tested). - **Notifications** (`planNotifications` in `src/gameplay/sim/reminders.ts`, `src/gameplay/data/notifications.json`): off until the player turns them on; each type can be switched off; none in quiet hours (they wait until morning); at most `maxPerDay` per local day.
- **Close calls** (`src/gameplay/sim/battle/closecall.ts`): "so close" notes after an attack, read from the finished battle and shown only when true. No rigged or faked near-misses, and no "almost" animations on random rewards: a chest only shows what it gave.
