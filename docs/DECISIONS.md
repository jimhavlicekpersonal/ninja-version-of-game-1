## Decisions

- **Identity:** display names come from `src/gameplay/data/*.json`. Internal ids are unchanged (`docs/rebuild/ID_MAP.md`). The app display name is "Kagemura". The bundle id is still the old one until new store listings exist (owner step); store product ids have their own names (`store.json` `storeProductIds`: `jade_pouch` … `season_scroll`), mapped from the game's unchanged ids.
- **Art:** all art is still generated in code (`src/presentation/art/ninja.ts`), with a look per level (re-audit: the Keep has 6 visual tiers and defenses 5, and every level inside a tier still adds a visible detail; tested in `tests/presentation/art/levels-and-sprites.test.ts`), roof materials by family (thatch, kawara tile, copper) and an original kamon set. Each of the 50 warriors has a role silhouette matching its badge, with a role ring in battle. Each building shows its job: resource by colour and pictogram (gold coin = coins, straw sheaf = rice), producers look busy (chimney smoke, furnace glow, water), stores look shut (namako lattice, locks, stacked bales), and noren curtains or signboards carry a mark (crossed blades = Dojo, hammer = Artisan's Hut, crest = Lodge). Resolution: two profiles (`render/quality.ts`): `high` (canvas up to 3x, buildings 2.5x, warriors/badges 3x, island 1.5x) and `low` (2x, 30 FPS); building levels are painted on first use, so a new village holds ~62 MB of textures on high and ~26 MB on low (measured in emulation; real memory is several times the texture figure, see the audit's re-audit record). Painted building art is capped per profile (`render/textureBudget.ts`, `paintedBudgetMB` 64 high / 24 low): scenes declare what they show (`holdBuildingTextures`), and the least recently used rest is removed and repainted when needed (report section 5 step 3). Mountains, fog, village life, ruins, hit flashes and tracers live in `render/fx.ts`; sound is koto/shamisen plucks and taiko (`audio/sound.ts`), haptics `audio/haptics.ts`. Fonts: M PLUS 1 for text and Dela Gothic One for titles and big labels (`--font-display`, `DISPLAY_FONT`; one weight, always 400), self-hosted in `src/presentation/ui/fonts/` (OFL). The plateau floats on a sea of clouds (`ground.ts`, `addSurroundings` in `render/assets.ts`). HUD icons are gradient SVGs whose ids `icon()` makes unique per copy. App icon and splash screens come from `npm run brand` (`tools/brand/brand.mjs`; needs Playwright). Real images override any asset key through `public/art/manifest.json`. `npm run art` shows a gallery. ART AI brief: `docs/art/ART_AI_BRIEF.md`.
- **Save format** is versioned (`src/gameplay/sim/save.ts`, now **v20**: purchase records keep what a Clan Stipend added (`stipend`), what a Season Scroll's premium claims gave (`premium`) and what a refund took (`refund`), so refunds take those back and reversed refunds give back exactly that (remnant audit 2026-11-06); v19: the 28-day calendar and the device's UTC offset for local task days; v18: a Clan Stipend keeps the Jade it was sold with (`stipend.jade`) and chests note the streak's own Jade; v17: refund debts, any resource may be negative; v16: Clan Stipend and goal events, purchase grants on records, new defense stats and adaptive task targets; v15: `profile.ageBand` (age bands); v14: `flags.sandbox` marks testing-sandbox villages; v13: Gang War chests collected; v12: donated-troop levels, research lanes, army presets, settled-season log, tasks fixed per period), with a migration step per version. Bump `SAVE_VERSION` for every state change. `repairTrainingQueue` runs on every load: one bad timer must never cost a player their village.
- **One writer per village:** every stored save carries a revision and `saveStore.save` refuses to write over one it didn't read (`SaveConflictError`), so a tab left open can't erase newer progress. The tab opened last owns the village; the other shows "Open somewhere else". A save the game can't read is kept (`backupDamaged`) and offered back at start-up and in Settings (`controller.backups`, `restoreBackup`). A save from a newer build stops the boot instead of being replaced.
- **Battle rules** are versioned (`SIM_RULES` in `sim/battle/battle.ts`, now 5: heroes (`BattleSetup.hero`, the Shadow Captain from Keep 7, report section 5 step 8); `SIM_RULES_COMPATIBLE` is [4] because a battle without a hero plays exactly as under 4; rules 4 (the design re-audit's role budgets and Shadow stealth) and rules 3 (relay strikes via `BattleSetup.startDamage`) are archived in `server/rules/v4` and `v3` so older games still play online; rules 2 is retired). Records carry the rules they were fought under; older replays are shown as unavailable, never replayed wrongly. `tests/gameplay/battle/rules-version.test.ts` fails when combat data changes without a bump.
- **Protocol** is v10 (servers accept 7; `match` needs 10). Ledger audit (`docs/audit/STATE_OF_THE_GAME.md` section 5 step 1, recorded in `docs/store/WORLDWIDE_AUDIT.md`): the battle seed comes from `POST /v1/attacks/start`, a started raid with no report is a loss at the next match after 8 minutes, a server-held village rerolls opponents only through a paid Next, raid reports lock both players, the under-18 cap is re-checked at redeem, imports above Keep 5 need account age, refunds leave debts in every resource, and passive war chests were cut. Report section 5 step 7 adds `rules-acceptance` (`PUT /v1/me/rules`: chat, Alliance/Gang names and renames need the current community rules accepted on the account, `403 rules_required`), App Store `RESCIND_CONSENT` (online play suspended for that account), friend requests by id under the search rules, names refusing contact handles, and deletion that purges stored answers and holds the name 35 days. Friendly Duels, Gangs, Gang Wars, relay strikes (rules 3), `503 busy` from the battle-checking queue. v8: game versions (`X-Data-Version`, tickets checked with the rules they were issued under, `supportedRules`/`minClientBuild` in hello, "Update Kagemura"), balance bundles, unique names and friends (`docs/PROTOCOL.md`). v7: the server owns the economy; every request carries `X-Protocol`, `X-Sim-Rules`, `X-Client-Build`, writes an `Idempotency-Key`; older games get `426 version_mismatch` and are told to update). v6 brought the audit fixes (donated levels, idempotent tickets, server-confirmed loot, legal bases and armies, equal war rosters).
- **Server (roadmap code steps 10–48 done; protocol 7 from step 26; `docs/backend/PVP_ROADMAP.md` marks each step, owner steps stay unticked with what was prepared):**
  - *Hardening (Phase 1):* the server issues each ticket's battle seed and refuses reports played with another; each base is stored once by content hash and replays are kept 30 days; database changes are numbered migrations run by `npm run server:migrate` as a release step (the server won't boot with pending ones); rate limits per IP, token and costly route (`429 rate_limited`, retryable for the client) and body limits; `DELETE /v1/me`, a web deletion page (`/account/delete`) and Settings → Account; reports, blocks, auto-hide after 3 reporters, chat mutes and a staff queue behind `ADMIN_TOKEN`.
  - *Scale and operations:* optional Redis (`REDIS_URL`) for the leaderboard sorted set, match locks (two attackers never get the same defender) and shared rate limits, through a small hand-written RESP client; JSON logs with request ids, Sentry envelopes without the SDK (server `SENTRY_DSN`, game `VITE_SENTRY_DSN` + Settings switch), `/healthz` and `/readyz`, Prometheus metrics behind `METRICS_TOKEN` (re-sim time, battles, desyncs from `BattleSubmission.clientHash`, errors by code). `npm run server:bench` measured re-simulation at 5 ms (Keep 1) to ~0.45 s p95 (Keeps 8–15) on a 2.1 GHz vCPU: a worker pool (roadmap step 57) is needed before real load.
  - *Server-owned village (Phase 2, steps 19–26):* the game moves its village to the server when it connects (`POST /v1/village/import`, refused if its Keep could not hold it; Settings says why), or starts a new one there. The game queues each economy command (`PLAYER_COMMANDS`, `sim/serverEconomy.ts`) with its time and `economyHash`; the server replays it with the same `applyCommand`, clamps times to its clock, and sends corrections when the hashes differ (the server wins). Attacks, war attacks, chests and gifts settle server-held villages in one transaction with the result, with an append-only ledger. Linked sign-ins (Apple, Google, Game Center, Play Games; `server/identity/verify.ts`) restore the account and its village on another device (cloud save). Purchases stay trusted from the device until Phase 4.
  - *Ranking (Phase 3, steps 27–32):* hidden Attack and Base Ratings (`sim/rating.ts`, `ranks.json` `rating`) pick the fairest base after the Keep window and power floor; bots never move a rating; a defender loses at most 150 honor a UTC day; a rebuilt layout restarts its Base Rating; a Power Ranking (skill plus Keep) beside the Honor leaderboard. Convergence is simulated in `tests/gameplay/balance/rating-convergence.test.ts`.
  - *Payments (Phase 4, steps 35–38):* the server checks every store receipt before delivery (`server/payments/apple.ts`: StoreKit 2 JWS against Apple's pinned root; `google.ts`: Play Developer API, consume or acknowledge, RTDN and Voided Purchases), credits each store transaction once, applies the under-18 cap across devices before the store is asked (purchase intents), and takes refunds back from a server-held village (`revokePurchase`; a reversed refund gives back what it took, `reinstatePurchase`). The game finishes a store transaction only after delivery; unfinished ones are delivered later; Store → Restore purchases. Remnant audit (2026-11-06): Google purchases are consumed by the server only when its own village holds them (acknowledged otherwise, so a lost answer or failed save on the device is delivered again); a receipt the server knows was refunded is refused; a credited purchase sent again after its village moved to the server is delivered there; and the game applies the server's refund records to its own village at start and on reconnection (`GameController.settleRefunds`; for a server-held village only the stipend, Scroll and looks, since the server owns the resources). Android purchases only in builds made with `VITE_PLAY_BILLING=1`.

- **Infiltration Points** (`src/gameplay/sim/battle/infiltration.ts`, `infiltration` in `battle.json`): warriors enter only inside the base's 6 points (map corners and edge midpoints, an opposite pair closed by the layout), and each point hides a third of the army's housing (minimum 15), so a full army comes in as at least three squads. Smoke Bombs still go anywhere. Scouting shows the points and the defenses covering each path to the Keep. Battles without `setup.infiltration` (records from before the rule) keep free placement. With it, a planned attack (mixed squads, chosen approach) beats a lazy one by about 9–29 points (Keeps 4–8) and best vs worst approach differ by 15–40 (`tests/gameplay/balance/placement.test.ts`). Defense damage ×1.3 and HP ×1.15 at every level, so a planned attack lands at ~60–80% and brute force falls short.
- **Ninja tactics:** Smoke Bomb (warriors *inside* the cloud can only be shot from defense range × 0.4, never below 2 tiles; 8 s, 1 + 1 per 40 housing charges, max 3, no stacking). It is taught as "smoke your warriors as they walk into tower range", which is what the rule rewards. Sabotage (Saboteur jams a defense 6 s; it is then immune for 10 s after recovering; one jam each). All numbers are in `battle.json` and `troops.json`.
- **Live defensive counters** (`src/gameplay/sim/battle/fortifications.ts`, `effect` in `buildings.json`): Paper Lantern Line (Keep 5, lights stealth/veiled warriors), Clockwork Repair Crane and Bitter Smoke Brazier (Keep 6, shorten jams, stuns and Hawk Tamer slowing / cut healing and auras), Kite-Cutter Ballista (Keep 8, a control tower: its bolt grounds a flyer 3 s, then 3 s immunity, and ground defenses finish it), Longbow Yagura (Keep 12, range 13, prefers attackers with range ≥ 3.5, blind inside 2 tiles). Soft: no stacking, short disables. A building being upgraded neither fires nor runs its effect, and scouting doesn't count it. Generated bases put the Crane and Incense next to the defenses they cover. Tests: `tests/gameplay/battle/counters.test.ts`, `audit-ai.test.ts`.

On top, defense levels get a measured mid-Keep bump (Keep 5 ×1.08, 6 ×1.15, 7–8 ×1.2 tapering to 12 ×1.04, 13–15 ×1.06; `tools/balance/midkeep.mjs`). Keep 7 was a hole (its bases fielded Keep 6 levels while armies gained two research levels and a new tier; every army scored ~100%): Keep 7 now unlocks Kunai Tower level 9, Shuriken Tower level 8 and Firework Mortar level 6, and the two towers' Keep 6 levels are ×1.5 so the step into the late curve is no longer ×2.17. - **Air pays for flying:** the Sky role's power budget is lower (efficiency 0.494, HP/DPS 2.7: air troops have 0.45× the HP and 0.75× the damage of the old numbers), because flyers skip walls and every ground-only defense; air used to win ~100% at every Keep. - **Range pays for safety:** Throwers deal 0.7× damage (efficiency 0.627, HP/DPS 3.43), because they fire from behind the front line and over walls (ranged mixes three-starred 69–100%). Warriors that still measured too strong carry a DPS tax (`dpsScale` in `roster.json`): Siege Bombardier 0.5 (and range 5, inside a Cannon's reach), Thousand Needle 0.6 (range 5), Hawk Tamer 0.6. See `docs/game-theory/PLAYABLE_ROSTER.md`.
- **Matchmaking follows earned power:** opponents are never weaker than the attacker's own strength (Keep and research progress on the Keep's level scale, where a newer tier's level 1 counts as 1 + `levelCapOffset`; `attackerStrength`) minus `powerSlack` 0.25 (`powerSlackByKeep`: 0.75 at Keeps 1-4, where a just-arrived army met near-capped bases of the Keep below and Keep 3 averaged 0.88 stars, and 0.5 at the measured dips of Keeps 8 and 12), so a maxed Keep 15 army meets Keep 15 bases even while its honor lags (the audit measured 97% three-star raids at Keep 15). `npm run balance:campaign` → `docs/game-theory/CAMPAIGN.md`.

## Engagement features (and their limits)

Built to bring players back for good reasons, within App Store rules, FTC guidance and loot-box laws. Keep these rules when changing them:

- **Daily chest** (`src/gameplay/sim/daily.ts`, `src/gameplay/data/daily.json`): free, one per UTC day, contents drawn from a weighted table whose exact odds are shown before opening. The draw comes from the village seed + day, so reloading can't reroll it. It is never sold: no Moonstone or real-money way to open, reopen or reroll it. If a paid random item is ever added, its odds must be shown before purchase (App Review Guideline 3.1.1) and the loot-box laws in each target market checked first.
- **Streak**: one step per day on a 7-step ladder of bigger chests (the top step's own Jade is paid once a week, report section 5 step 6). A missed day never wipes it: a free weekly skip covers one day, otherwise you move back one step per missed day. Skips and streak repairs are never sold.
- **Limited-time events** (`src/gameplay/sim/events.ts`, `src/gameplay/data/events.json`): a fixed schedule repeating every 14 days. Start and end times come only from the schedule; nothing resets or extends them, so every countdown is real. Offers keep their deadline too (tested).
- **Notifications** (`planNotifications` in `src/gameplay/sim/reminders.ts`, `src/gameplay/data/notifications.json`): off until the player turns them on; each type can be switched off; none in quiet hours (they wait until morning); at most `maxPerDay` per local day.
- **Close calls** (`src/gameplay/sim/battle/closecall.ts`): "so close" notes after an attack, read from the finished battle and shown only when true. No rigged or faked near-misses, and no "almost" animations on random rewards: a chest only shows what it gave.
- The model player opens the chest daily and plays through events, so pacing (`tests/gameplay/balance/progression.test.ts`) includes this income.
