# Army balance (generated)

Generated by `npm run balance:armies` (`tools/balance/armyLab.ts`). Do not edit by hand.

Equal-housing armies (each fills the Keep's full camp space, troops at the Keep's research cap) fight generated bases with the bot plan: one side, toughest troops first. 3 seeds × 6 layouts (random, kunai_ring, walled_keep, mortar_core, twin_gates, open_court) per cell. Bases are at the same Keep with mid-level buildings.

A composition takes the three newest unlocked troops of each family it uses:

- **Mixed (raid bot mix)** (`mixed`): the raid bots' mix for that Keep
- **Melee rush** (`melee`): 100% melee
- **Artillery-heavy** (`artillery`): 70% artillery, 30% melee
- **Support-heavy** (`support`): 40% support, 60% melee
- **Air** (`air`): 80% air, 20% melee
- **Shadow strike** (`shadow`): 70% shadow, 30% melee
- **Siege** (`siege`): 50% siege, 50% melee
