# Shadow Clans: from one player to a PvP ecosystem

Owner: GAME THEORY (rules and numbers in `src/gameplay/sim/world/` and `src/gameplay/data/{clans,world,competition}.json`), with matching BACKEND (`server/core/shadow.ts`, protocol 4) and GUI (`src/presentation/ui/worldUi.ts`) parts.

**The principle:** the game is a complete single-player game, and real players gradually take slots that AI clans fill today. AI villages and real villages use the same battle engine, troop and building stats, economy, layout rules and server re-simulation. Nothing needs a minimum population: with zero other players every system still works.

## Numbers (all in data)

- `clans.json`: clans (incl. signature warriors), bands (`below 1`, `above 2`, `challengeAbove 1`), sim (12 h steps, start spread 0.5, +0.06 strength per step, −0.25 on defeat, 36 h rebuild, revenge 2-20 h later for 24 h, expand 35% × aggression, reclaim 25% after 48 h), generator (5 attempts, ±20%, 50 layouts per clan), treasury (full = 3 raids of loot, +8% per step, loot ×0.6-1.3), rivals board (`rivalAim`, `challengeStrengthAbove`), the first-raid outpost layout.
- `world.json`: provinces with terrain (`ringScale`), settlement kinds, conquest stars (2), tribute (12% of the Keep's minimum raid loot per Shrine per day), faction ranks, signature-warrior rank and share, missions and war.
- `competition.json`: Keep titles, PvP gates, rating weights and scale, population table, capacity, candidate pool and rating band, Shadow Rank (Elo scale 400, K 32, 48 for the first 10 games, floor 100, 28-day seasons, keep 50%), tournament, catch-up, the Alliance board, war formats (and which fight AI war bands), regions, leadership, health targets.

## Pacing

The model player (`tests/gameplay/balance/progression.test.ts`, four short sessions a day) reaches Keep 3 in about an hour, Keep 4 within the first day, Keep 5 in about 2.2 days and Keep 6 in about 4.2 days, against the brief's proposed 1 hour, 4 hours, 1 day and 3 days. The Keep cost curve is ×3.1 per level (rule #15 still holds), and large Jade top-ups are steeper so rushing a Keep still costs more than the starting Jade (#20). What still separates the model from the brief's numbers is its mid-game flow (it raids about four times a day and builds storage and extras as it goes); a player who plays more often gets there sooner.

## Honesty rules (kept)

AI villages, raiders, factions and alliance members are always labelled AI. Real players are labelled PLAYER. Rewards are fixed, shown in advance and never sold (tribute, missions, wars, tournament thresholds, season rewards). Shadow Rank never moves for battles against the computer.
