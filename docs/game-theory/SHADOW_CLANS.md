# Shadow Clans: from one player to a PvP ecosystem

Owner: GAME THEORY (rules and numbers in `src/gameplay/sim/world/` and `src/gameplay/data/{clans,world,competition}.json`), with matching BACKEND (`server/core/shadow.ts`, protocol 4) and GUI (`src/presentation/ui/worldUi.ts`) parts.

**The principle:** the game is a complete single-player game, and real players gradually take slots that AI clans fill today. AI villages and real villages use the same battle engine, troop and building stats, economy, layout rules and server re-simulation. Nothing needs a minimum population: with zero other players every system still works.

## Numbers (all in data)

- `clans.json`: clans, bands (`below 1`, `above 2`, `challengeAbove 1`), sim (12 h steps, +0.06 strength per step, −0.25 on defeat, 36 h rebuild, revenge 2-20 h later for 24 h, expand 35% × aggression, reclaim 25% after 48 h), generator (5 attempts, ±20%), first raid.
- `world.json`: provinces, settlement kinds, conquest stars (2), tribute (12% of the Keep's minimum raid loot per Shrine per day), faction ranks, missions and war.
- `competition.json`: Keep titles, PvP gates, rating weights, population table and capacity, Shadow Rank (K 32, 48 for the first 10 games, floor 100, 28-day seasons, keep 50%), tournament, catch-up, war formats, regions, leadership, health targets.

## Pacing

The model player (`tests/gameplay/balance/progression.test.ts`) fights every raid as a real battle against the new clan villages. It still lands inside the existing targets: Keep 2 at 4 min, Keep 3 at 54 min, Keep 4 at 0.7 days, Keep 5 at 4.2 days, **Keep 6 at 13.6 days** (was 14.2). The brief proposes faster later levels (Keep 5 in 1 day, Keep 6 in 3 days). That was **not** changed: twenty balance-pass tests pin today's curve (hall time ×2.7 per level, cost multipliers, paybacks). Compressing it is a separate balance change; catch-up already exists for when Keep 7+ launch.

## Honesty rules (kept)

AI villages, raiders, factions and alliance members are always labelled AI. Real players are labelled PLAYER. Rewards are fixed, shown in advance and never sold (tribute, missions, wars, tournament thresholds, season rewards). Shadow Rank never moves for battles against the computer.

## Not done yet (next steps)

1. **Shared territory online.** Each player's world map is generated from their own seed. The server assigns regions and counts faction members, but human-held settlements do not yet appear on other players' maps. That needs a region world-state service (settlement ownership, conflicts) on top of `regions`.
2. **Faction strategy by humans.** Human leaders are chosen with safeguards, but they have no powers yet (e.g. choosing the weekly war target).
3. **Combat rating storage.** `P` is implemented and tested; the server bands by Keep window and trophies and orders real candidates by offense vs defense. Storing `P` per player for broader bands is a later step.
4. **Cross-player dashboard.** Day-1/7/30 retention across players needs server-side analytics collection (the client keeps events on the device).
5. **Pacing proposal** above, and Keep levels 7-10 content.
