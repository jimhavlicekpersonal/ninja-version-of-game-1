# Campaigns (generated)

Generated by `npm run balance:campaign` (`tools/balance/campaign.ts`). Do not edit by hand. The same numbers are in `campaign.json`, which `tests/gameplay/balance/campaign-gates.test.ts` holds to lower and upper bounds (and fails when they are stale).

The model player (`tools/balance/playerModel.ts`) plays real timers, earned resources, real battles and offline raids with the simple bot attack plan (a human usually does better). Opponents are matched by honor, but never below the attacker's earned strength minus the Keep's `powerSlackByKeep` (else `powerSlack`). Army strength is the Keep the army's research matches (`attackerStrength`: a Keep 12 army two levels behind its caps reads about 10.5); Honor is the average honor while raiding at that Keep. Loot is Acorns + Dew banked per raid.

Defense columns: raids against the village at that Keep (real offline bot raids against the real layout), the share held (no star lost), Acorns + Dew taken per raid, and the honor change per raid.
