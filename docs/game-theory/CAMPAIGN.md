Generated by `npm run balance:campaign` (`tools/balance/campaign.ts`). Do not edit by hand.

The model player (`tools/balance/playerModel.ts`) plays real timers, earned resources, real battles and offline raids with the simple bot attack plan (a human usually does better). Opponents are matched by honor, but never below the attacker's earned strength minus `powerSlack`. Army strength is the Keep the army's research matches (`attackerStrength`: a Keep 12 army two levels behind its caps reads about 10.5); Honor is the average honor while raiding at that Keep.
