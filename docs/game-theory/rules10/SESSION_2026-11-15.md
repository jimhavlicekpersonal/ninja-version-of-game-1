## Corrections to the v5 report and the plan

1. **The breach radius does nothing measurable.** v5 said the breach rule made siege a strong partner for shadows.
   Tested at Keeps 9 and 15 with the radius at 2, 1 and 0.5 tiles: the scores are the same to the point. Breaches fire
   (about 90 in 24 siege raids at Keep 12) but opening a wider hole does not change a raid. What gave siege its job
   is the wall damage multiplier and walls with four times the health. `kit.breachRadius` can be dropped.
   The strong shadow + siege mixes are a composition effect: the siege warriors were tuned up to make a weak showcase
   army average, and better partners then overshoot.
2. **Plan step B2 is wrong.** Combat numbers never ship in balance bundles, so the battle side of Rules 10 cannot be
   switched on by a bundle. It needs `npm run rules:snapshot`, a SIM_RULES bump and the server first, or the
   `?sandbox&preview` path. Only the raid and economy numbers could go in a bundle.

## The automatic tune and search loop (plan steps A2, A3): `tune6.py`

Each round fights every army against the six layouts in four tunings on bases not used before, searches 40 random
armies against the defender's best mix, lets the defender respond, adds any found army more than 4 points above the
target as a ceiling, and steps. The objective changed from "every showcase army equal" to a role frontier: no army
above the target, and for every role the best army that leans on it at the target.

Keep 12, six rounds, then scored on 48 fresh bases (Rules 10 → after the loop):

| Test from the plan | Rules 10 | After the loop | Pass at |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rise in the game's value when the attacker may use every found army | +3.3 | +0.8 | 2 or less |
| Strongest found army above the best standard army | +1 | 0 | 4 or less |
| Two searches in a row find nothing new | no | yes (rounds 4 and 5) | yes |
| Bases where all flyers are the best answer | 33% | 17% | 25% or less |
| Gap between easiest and hardest layout | 11 | 7 | 10 or less |
| Siege against Walled Keep, against its own average | +8 | +3 | -3 or better |
| Gap between best and worst standard army | 11 | 19 | 12 or less |
| Weakest standard army | healers + guardians 58 | healers + guardians 51 | within 8 of the mean |

The loop closed the search, flyer and layout tests at Keep 12 and opened the standard-army gap: the frontier objective
does not hold up a composition that is not its role's best, and healers with guardians fell. The next version needs
both: the frontier, plus a floor under each standard army. The support kit redesign (plan step A5) is the real fix.

Keep 9 ran two rounds (found armies at 74 to 83; state saved in `S11.json`). Keep 15 was not started.
