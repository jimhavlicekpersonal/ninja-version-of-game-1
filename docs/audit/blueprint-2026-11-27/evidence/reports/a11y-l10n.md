# Accessibility, localization quality, fonts, formats and content honesty: audit

Read-only audit of `/home/user/ninja-version-of-game-1` (2026-11-27). Scripts, raw output and screenshots: `(session scratch, not kept)/a11y/` (load.js, plural.js, ident.js, ident2.js, artefacts.js, arrows.js, digits.js, trail.js, legacy.js, terms.js, read.js, canvasclip.js + canvasclip.json, a11y.js + a11y.json, kb2.js, shots/). No tracked file was changed; the dev server on port 5313 is stopped; `git status` is clean.

## 1. What was covered

- **Catalogs by machine**: all 49 target locales x 5 namespaces (ui 377, data 790, boot 17, messages 2127, pages 338 = 3649 entries each) against `locales/en`: ICU plural categories vs `Intl.PluralRules`; identical to English; identical between 14 related pairs (hi/mr, nb/da, ms/id, hr/sl, cs/sk, ru/uk, es/pt, es/ca, zh-Hans/zh-Hant, sv/nb, sv/da, ur/ar, bn/hi); translator artefacts; edge spaces, end punctuation, colons; digits written in text; arrows; old-game vocabulary in 20 languages plus store listings, `public/` and `dist/`; one term translated two ways (161 groups of identical short English).
- **By reading**: 34 high-stakes strings (withdrawal waiver, real-money warning, age gate, under-18 cap, refund and debt, Apple refund consent, statistics consent, account deletion, terms gate, tutorial) in es, fr, de, pt, ru, ja, zh-Hans, ko, ar, hi = 340 strings. I read these as a fluent reader, not as a native legal reviewer.
- **Browser (Chromium + Playwright, dev build, sandbox save)**: contrast of every visible text node on 8 surfaces (village, Army, Store, Daily, News, Settings, Builders, Keep sheet) at 1280x800; control names; Tab order and focus ring; keyboard-only build; `forced-colors` emulation; Arabic Army panel at 360x640; Phaser canvas label metrics in 27 languages.
- **Code read**: `locales/runtime.ts`, `src/presentation/ui/i18n.ts`, `format.ts`, `render/fonts.ts`, `styles.css` (media queries, focus, button colours), `battleHud.ts` key handler, `sim/monetization.ts` cap message.
- **Store text**: `docs/store/LISTING.md` and the 49 localized `LISTING.md` against `docs/STATUS.md`.
- **Not done**: audio (section E) entirely; a full grep of hand-built formats (section B) beyond what is reported; colour-only signals beyond the recorded A-3 check; screen-reader runs; anything on a physical device.

## 3. Findings

### LOC-1 Canvas labels clip tall and deep scripts (MEDIUM)

- **Where**: `src/presentation/render/BattleScene.ts:633` (Infiltration Point labels), `:968` (ability and counter call-outs), `src/presentation/render/VillageScene.ts:240-250` (build timers), `:405-412` (floating "Done!", "Level N!", "+500"), `src/presentation/render/fx.ts:332` (stamps). None sets `padding`, `testString` or `metrics`.
- **Why**: Phaser 3 sizes a `Text` canvas from the ink of the Latin test string `|MÉqgy` plus the stroke. A glyph that rises above É or drops below g/q/y is cut by the canvas edge; the only margin is half the stroke (2 px at 13 px text).
- **Measured** (Chromium, the game's `BODY_FONT`, 13 px bold, stroke 4 = the call-out style; Phaser metrics came out ascent 13, descent 3-4). 57 canvas strings per language (55 ability names, "Done!", "Level 7!", the point label, three timers); count whose real ink (`actualBoundingBoxAscent/Descent`) exceeds Phaser's metrics, and the worst overshoot:

|---|---|---|---|
| ar | 48 | 5.9 px above | `mirage_twin.ability.name` "الطُّعم": ink ascent 18.9 vs 13; 17 opaque pixels on the canvas's first row |
| ml | 37 | 4.2 px below | `armored_guardian.ability.name` "ഉറച്ചുനിൽക്കുക": descent 7.2 vs 3 |
| kn | 24 | 4.2 px below | `trap_breaker.ability.name` "ನಿಶ್ಶಸ್ತ್ರಗೊಳಿಸು": descent 7.2 vs 3 |

- **Limits**: the sandbox draws these scripts with DejaVu Sans, FreeSans and Loma, not the phones' Noto fonts, so the amounts will differ per device; the cause does not. Up to 2 px costs only the outline; above that the letter's own ink is cut (ar, ml, kn, bn, pa, or). The last canvas row also carries the drop shadow in every language, so the table uses the metric comparison, not "ink on the last row".
- **Not known**: ARABIC.md and HEBREW.md only list "canvas labels were not checked for shaping on devices"; UX-08 is about canvas text size.
- **Fix**: one helper for all five call sites that adds a script sample to `testString` by `data-script` (Arabic "لُّ", Thai "ปู่", Malayalam "ക്ഷൂ", Devanagari "कृ"), or `padding: { top: 4, bottom: 4 }` for non-Latin scripts with the origin corrected; plus a test that compares each call-out string's ink with the metrics for every ready language.

  2. `ui.your_collectors_gathered_55d534315d` "Your collectors gathered " -> "جمعت مُجمِّعاتك"; `hud.ts:2429` appends the list ("جمعت مُجمِّعاتك500 عملة").

  4. `ui.this_device_still_holds_your_online_ac_8b04a785ce` "... في" and `ui.deleting_from_another_device_or_the_we_2bf7af1665` "... على"; `hud.ts:1397`, `hud.ts:1232` append a URL. `pages.json` `pages.account.23`/`.24` "مرجعك:" + the deletion reference number.

### A11Y-1 Button and small-text contrast (MEDIUM)

- **Primary and sky buttons**: `src/presentation/ui/styles.css:286` `.btn.primary { background: linear-gradient(#90e870 0%, #5aa85e 52%, #3f8a4b 53%, ...); color: #fff }` and `:289` `.btn.sky { ... #a8e4ff 0%, #4f93c8 52%, #3f7fb5 53% ...; color: #fff }`. Labels are 13-15 px weight 800 (not "large text"), so WCAG 1.4.3 asks 4.5:1. White on the band behind the label: green #5aa85e 2.9:1 and #3f8a4b 4.2:1; blue #4f93c8 3.3:1 and #3f7fb5 4.3:1. The top stop is 1.5:1 and 1.4:1 (what the script reports as worst case). The 1 px text shadow helps the eye, not the ratio.
  Affected in the run: the Store's price buttons ("$0.99 (test)", 8 nodes), "Claim day 1", the Army panel's "+1" and "Train".
- **Small HUD text** (solid or near-solid backgrounds, measured): `.badge` white on #ea6a52, 12 px/800: 3.14:1 (`styles.css:475`); `.army-pill.full` white on #4f9a5b, 12 px/800: 3.44:1 (`:679`); `.res-cap` #5f6b78 on rgb(234,220,191), 12 px/700: 4.01:1 (`:135`); `.muted.small` #5f6b78 on #efe3c9 (the paper gradient's darkest stop), 12 px/500: 4.27:1 (`:250`; raid rule, Keep seal, store note, dojo speed, timestamps).
- **Not counted**: the Attack and Build round buttons (label has a dark stroke on a gradient, not measurable by ratio); `.btn.gold` (dark text, about 8:1 at the label band).
- **Not known**: the lists only say "not tested for colour-blind contrast" (WORLDWIDE_AUDIT.md:428) and pass A-3 colour-blind signals; no ratio was recorded.
- **Fix**: darken the two gradients' label band (for example green #2f7a3c -> 5.3:1, blue #2f6aa3 -> 5.7:1) or use dark text; set `--ink-soft` to about #55606c (5.0:1 on #efe3c9); badge background #c9432f (4.6:1); add a contrast check to the Playwright surface tool.

### LOC-3 Bangla mixes Bengali and Western digits (MEDIUM)

- `locales/runtime.ts:23` (`numberText`), `src/presentation/ui/i18n.ts:120,124` and `format.ts:17` force `numberingSystem: 'latn'`: every amount, count and percentage the game formats is Western (`Intl.NumberFormat('bn', {numberingSystem:'latn'}).format(1234567)` = "12,34,567").
- `locales/bn/*.json` writes Bengali digits in 175 strings, following `docs/localization/BANGLA.md:23`, which assumes the opposite ("`Intl` formats `bn` with Bengali digits ... Numbers written inside the text use Bengali digits too ... Bangla needed no code change").
- Examples: `ui.json` `trials.cleared` "পার হয়েছে: ধাপ ১ থেকে {best}" renders "ধাপ ১ থেকে 12" (both sets in one sentence); `calendar.title` "২৮ দিনের ক্যালেন্ডার" over a grid of Western day numbers; `offers.minor` "১৮ বছরের কম..."; `store.calendarLook`; `raid.rejected.expiredAt` "৭২ ঘণ্টার সীমার". The Bangla store listing uses Bengali digits throughout (no Western digit at all).
- The checker guards Arabic only (`easternArabicDigits`, `lib.mjs:176`). Marathi has 1 such string (`ui.json` `raid.rejected.expiredAt` "७२ तासांच्या"), Gujarati 1 (`whatsNew.0_2_0`).
- **Fix**: choose per language. Either convert the 175 strings to Western digits (as Hindi, Marathi, Gujarati and Punjabi do), or stop forcing `latn` for `bn` everywhere including canvas text; extend the digit rule in the checker to every script with its own digits; correct BANGLA.md.

### LOC-6 Bare "$" in the under-18 cap refusals (MEDIUM)

- `locales/en/messages.json:2098` `srv.purchase.minor_cap` "Monthly spending limit of ${limit} reached for players under 18. Nothing was charged." and `:2099` `srv.purchase.over_cap_held` "This purchase would go past the ${limit} monthly limit ..."; `sim.monthly_spending_limit_of_reached_for__5ea76bb591`; built in `src/gameplay/sim/monetization.ts:160` as `` `$${cap.toFixed(2)}` ``. All ten languages read keep the bare sign (es "de ${limit}", de "von ${limit}", ja "月間上限 ${limit}", fr "{limit} $").
- The cap is in US-dollar list prices. Settings says so (`economyUi.ts:710`, "Amounts are the items' US-dollar list prices") and formats it with `Intl.NumberFormat(..., { currency: 'USD' })` (`economyUi.ts:29`, giving "US$" where needed). The refusal a minor actually sees does neither.
- **Why it matters**: Spanish is served to Mexico, Argentina, Chile and Colombia, where "$100" means pesos (about 5 US dollars in Mexico); English to Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Singapore; Traditional Chinese to Taiwan and Hong Kong. A child or parent reads the wrong limit in the one message about why a paid purchase was not delivered. Beast Mode marks "Intl numbers, dates and currency" PASS on the Settings line only.
- **Fix**: send the cap as a number and format it on the device with the same `usd()` formatter, and word the message "... of {limit} (US-dollar list price)". Drop `toFixed`.

### A11Y-2 Keyboard-only play stops at the map (MEDIUM)

- **What works** (1280x800, no mouse): Tab reaches all 23 HUD buttons with a 3 px focus ring; Enter on Build opens the dialog and moves focus into it; choosing a card with Enter starts placement with DOM buttons "Cancel" and "Place here", and a Coin Mint was placed at the offered spot during the key sequence (buildings 36 -> 37). Dialogs trap Tab and close on Escape (`hud.ts:2644`).
- **What does not**:
  - The map canvas has `role="img"` and no `tabindex`; `VillageScene.ts` and `hud.ts` have no key handler for the map (grep for `keydown`/`keyboard.on`). An existing building cannot be selected, so its sheet (Upgrade, Collect, Move, Finish now) is out of reach unless a chip happens to point at it; a placement tile cannot be chosen.
  - After a card is chosen, focus falls to `<body>` instead of the place bar (`afterPick.active = BODY`); the next Tab goes to the top bar.
  - In battle, `battleHud.ts:1091-1099`: S clicks the ability card and 1-9 click a warrior card; nothing deploys. A raid needs a pointer on the map.
  - The canvas's own label says "Every action is also on the buttons around the map." (`aria-label`, read out by screen readers). That is not true for upgrading or collecting from a chosen building, nor for deploying.
- **Known part skipped**: the Honor pill without `tabindex` (ui-surface.md:88) is fixed now (`BUTTON.res-trophies` is in the Tab order).
- **Limits**: one run on the sandbox village; I did not try every panel for an indirect route to a building's sheet.
- **Fix**: a "Buildings" list (or arrow keys with a focus cursor on the map) that opens any building's sheet; arrow keys to move the placement ghost and focus on "Place here"; in battle, Enter or a "send through point A/B/C" button for the armed card. Until then, correct the canvas label.

### A11Y-3 Windows contrast themes (LOW)

- `styles.css` (1245 lines) has no `@media (forced-colors: active)` and no `prefers-contrast` rule (grep: 0).
- Emulated `forcedColors: 'active'` at 1280x800 (screenshot `shots/forced-colors.png`): the building sheet's body loses its panel (text sits on the map with only per-line backplates, the sheet shows as three separate pieces); the build progress bar is an empty white track (the fill is a background colour, which the mode removes) next to "Building · 8s left"; "Finish now", "Cancel" and "Move" have no outline; resource pills lose their shape and the storage bars vanish (`.res` border 0, background none). The round rail buttons keep a border.
- **Fix**: a short `@media (forced-colors: active)` block: `border: 1px solid ButtonText` on `.btn`, `.res`, `.sheet`, `.modal`; bar fills with `background: Highlight; forced-color-adjust: none`.

### LOC-4 Dates in native digits (LOW)

- `src/presentation/ui/i18n.ts:126-128` `dateText` and `locales/runtime.ts` `utcText` call `Intl.DateTimeFormat(code, ...)` without a numbering system. Node/ICU 78 and Chromium resolve `mr` to `deva` and `bn` to `beng`: "२७ नोव्हें, २०२६, २:०५ PM", while MARATHI.md:31 states Western digits and every other number is Western. All other 47 locales resolve to `latn` (Thai's Buddhist year is by design, THAI.md).
- **Fix**: pass `numberingSystem: 'latn'` (or the per-language choice from LOC-3) to both formatters.

### LOC-5 Urdu arrows (LOW)

- Arabic and Hebrew replaced all 51 "→" with "←" (an arrow is not mirrored by the bidirectional algorithm, so "3 → 4" in a right-to-left line points from 4 back to 3). Urdu flipped 47 and kept 4 in `locales/ur/messages.json`: `ui.lv_99eff11c93` "لیول {arg1} → {arg2}", `ui.level_ab12f81e7d` "{arg1} → لیول {arg2}", `ui.hp_damage_f5da8dfe3f` "صحت {arg1} → {arg2} · نقصان {arg3} → {arg4}", `ui.ago_e411ba9ec3`.
- **Fix**: "←" in those four; add arrow direction to the checker for `dir: rtl`.

### LOC-7 Portuguese term drift in consent and age texts (LOW)

- Village is "vila" in 170 Portuguese strings and "aldeia" in 4: `ui.json` `terms.body` ("Sua aldeia continua funcionando neste dispositivo"), `consent.apple.detail` ("Nunca seu nome, sua aldeia ou seu chat"), `messages.json` `service.online_play_not_available_under13`, `srv.age.under13_offline`. Device is "aparelho" in 74 and "dispositivo" in 5. The same four strings are the terms gate, the Apple refund consent and the under-13 notice.
- **Fix**: "vila" and "aparelho" in those strings; add both pairs to `locales/pt/TERMS.md`.

## 4. Checked and found sound

- **ICU plurals**: 55 English plural messages; every locale carries every category `Intl.PluralRules` asks for (pl, uk, cs, sk, sl, hr, he, ro, ru, ar included). Only "many" is absent in es/pt/fr/it/ca (exact millions; `other` reads the same) and "zero" in 4 Arabic messages (falls to `other`). No plural was flattened. Placeholder parity is enforced by the checker and holds.
- **Translator artefacts**: 0 double spaces, whole-value quotes, HTML entities, `[object Object]`/`undefined`/`NaN`, leftover keys, ASCII `...`, emoji differences. The 129 "note/TODO" hits are Spanish/Portuguese "todo"; the 4 "mojibake" hits are real Vietnamese/Turkish "Â". Greek uses ";" for "?" correctly; Thai drops final periods by design; Amharic "፦" for ":".
- **Identical to English**: 7-12 per non-Latin locale, 40-120 per Latin one: brands, names, script names, cognates. No untranslated sentence.
- **Related languages**: no copied sentences. hi/mr 0 long identical strings, bn/hi 0, ur/ar 0, ru/uk 1 (a plural of one shared word), es/pt 1, hr/sl 1; nb/da 20, ms/id 16, zh-Hans/zh-Hant 13 and cs/sk 5 are short plural or symbol strings that are correct in both.
- **Old-game vocabulary**: none in any locale's values, any `docs/store/*/LISTING.md` or `LOCALE.json`, `public/*.html` or `public/*/*.html`; `dist/` has "toadstool" only in ids and keys.
- **Term consistency**: in es, fr, de, pt, ja, zh-Hans, ko, ru, ar, hi the core terms (Jade, Honor, Provisions, Alliance, Gang, Keep, Shield, Store, Shinobi...) have one translation each; the only splits are case forms (ru "Нефрит/Нефрита", es/fr/pt "de Jade").
- **Reader pass, 340 strings**: withdrawal waiver, real-money warning, age gate, "we keep only an age group", refund/debt, Apple consent, statistics consent, deletion and terms gate carry the English meaning in all ten languages; legal terms are the right ones (es "derecho de desistimiento", fr "droit de rétractation", de "Widerrufsrecht", pt "direito de arrependimento", ko "청약 철회권"); register is consistent (tú, tu, du, você, ты, です・ます, 해요체, आप; Arabic verbal nouns). - **Store listings**: the English listing's claims match `docs/STATUS.md` (fifty warriors, Keep 1-15, one hero named, no "offline" selling point). None of the 49 localized listings mentions offline play or more than the Shadow Captain; all carry Keep 15, Keep 7 and the under-18 line.
