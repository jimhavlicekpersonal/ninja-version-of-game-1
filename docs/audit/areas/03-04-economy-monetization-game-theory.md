Scope: data in `src/gameplay/data/*.json`, sim in `src/gameplay/sim/`, server in `server/core/`, `src/presentation/ui/economyUi.ts`, `tools/balance/`, generated docs. Arithmetic scripts: `(session scratchpad, not in the repo)/agent-econ/{econ,troops,research}.mjs`. `curves`, `topup`, `progression` and `policies` balance tests run and pass (36 s). No repo files were changed.

## A. Monetization funnel and pricing

**E01 — HIGH — The Starter Pack (first-purchase offer) is a one-shot trigger that is silently lost in common cases. VERIFIED.**
`src/gameplay/sim/village.ts:491-493`: `first_long_wait` fires once (`flags.longWaitSeen = true` is set *before* the offer is checked). `sim/offers.ts:16,37` refuses the offer when the player has not yet stated "adult" (`profile.under18 === null` until a purchase attempt or first online connection, `economyUi.ts:127`, `connection.ts:559`), when the account is < 24 h old (`offers.json minAccountHours`), or when `controller.ts:262` already showed this session's one offer (`maxPerSession: 1`). `all_builders_busy` (Artisan's Bundle) fires on nearly every timed upgrade with two busy builders (`village.ts:496`), so in the session where the first > 1 h timer (usually the Keep 5 upgrade, day ~4 regular / ~12 casual) starts, the Bundle usually takes the session slot and the Starter Pack is gone forever; every offline/undeclared-age player loses it too. Fix: do not consume `longWaitSeen` unless the offer was actually activated; re-arm per session; let Starter outrank Bundle; or trigger from account age rather than the first timer.

**E02 — HIGH — Offers only exist for players who have already said "adult"; the pre-purchase funnel is dead for everyone else. VERIFIED.** `offersAllowed` (`offers.ts:16`) requires `under18 === false && ageBand === 'adult'`; age is asked only at a purchase attempt (`economyUi.ts:127`) or before going online (`connection.ts:773`). A native player who plays offline or declines the age screen (`ageDeclined`) never sees Starter/Bundle/War Supplies. Under-18 bands never see offers at all (defensible legally, but then the Store tab "Offers" is permanently empty for them: say so). Suggest asking age at tutorial end (neutral screen already exists) so offers can run.

**E03 — HIGH — The Clan Stipend undercuts the whole pack ladder and carries no retention hook. VERIFIED.** `store.json`: 30 × 20 = 600 Jade for $2.99 = **200.7 Jade/$** vs packs 45.5 ($0.99) → 75.0 ($99.99) Jade/$; the $9.99 Box gives exactly the same 600 Jade for 3.3× the price. Missed days are never lost (`stipend.ts`), it is rebuyable the moment it is empty, so a rational spender's ceiling is ~$3/month and the stipend does not drive daily logins (industry monthly cards earn their discount by requiring the login). Suggest: keep it consumer-friendly but reduce to ~10–12 Jade/day, or make it 30 calendar days, and/or give the stipend a small daily extra only when claimed that day.

**E04 — MEDIUM — Pack ladder has no anchor, decoy or "most popular" structure; bonus chips are computed against the $0.99 pack. VERIFIED.** `economyUi.ts packsGrid`: `+N% value` relative to `packs[0]` (Pinch): +10/+23/+32/+43/+54/+65 %. Monotonic and honest, but $1.99 → $4.99 → $9.99 all land within 50–60 Jade/$ (little reason to step up) and nothing tells the player what 600 Jade *does* (e.g. "finishes a 2-day upgrade"). Suggest Jade-per-$ framing in game terms and one clearly best mid pack.

**E05 — MEDIUM — The skip curve is ~2× typical genre prices in dollars while the economy also hands out ~500 free Jade/month. VERIFIED (computed).** `skipCurve`: 1 h = 25, 24 h = 300, 7 d = 1,200 Jade (≈ $5/day, $20/week at the $9.99 rate; CoC ≈ $2.6/day). Free faucets for a daily player (script `research.mjs`): streak +5/day after step 7, chest EV 0.95/day, free pass track 150/season, bot-Alliance-War EV ~25/week (see E12), Bounty Hunt 35/14 d, league chest 10–200/month, achievements 2,540 one-time, obstacles 26 × 2–6 → **≈ 540 Jade/month without Trials or Gang Wars**, plus 500 starting. That is the $9.99 Box every month for free, and it is *more* than the stipend. Result: cheap builders are covered by free Jade (3rd hut 250 on day 1, 4th 500 by month 2), and the big sinks (skips) are priced so high that free Jade is the rational source. Pick one: trim passive faucets (E12, E13) or lower skip prices and let payers buy time.

**E06 — MEDIUM — Rushing the Keep is both the cheapest large Jade sink and actively harmful to the buyer ("paying makes matchmaking worse"). VERIFIED (computed).** Top-up + skip for every Keep level 1→15 ≈ 40.9k + 6.7k ≈ **47.6k Jade ≈ $635** (`econ.mjs`). Nothing else gates the Keep (collectors/camps/research do not). Matchmaking then uses `ownHall ± 1` and `attackerStrength ≥ hall − 1.5` (`matchmaking.ts:124-141`, `ranks.json hallsBelow/Above 1, researchFloorKeeps 1.5`): a rushed Keep 15 with level-1 research meets Keep 14–15 bases with level-1 warriors, and as a defender faces full Keep 14–16-cap bot armies (`attackerFor`, mirror) with Keep-1 defenses and a 9.6 M loot cap per raid. Also `curves.test` only requires `rush(5) ≥ 3 × starting Jade`. Suggest a soft gate (Keep upgrade requires N buildings at level, as in the genre) and/or matchmaking by `powerRating` not Keep alone.

**E07 — MEDIUM — Season Scroll value vs. packs is fine but its Jade depends on a 19-day perfect-task schedule and a global season clock. VERIFIED.** Premium Jade = 100+80+80+80 = 340 for $4.99 (vs 280 Jade Bag) plus Keep-scaled resources and a skin; 30 tiers × 300 pts need 9,000 pts at `passPointsPerDay` = 471 → 19.1 days of all three dailies plus weeklies. Honest "≈ N Jade value" line exists (`passPremiumValue`). Note premium tier *resources* are claimed at the Keep at claim time (`passTierReward` → `scaleReward(hallLevel)`): a player who delays claims until after a Keep upgrade gets 1.5–2× more (small, legal timing exploit; also applies to `claimTier` free track).

**E08 — LOW — Store product table in `docs/SHIPPING.md` 3b omits `jade_pinch` ($0.99) and `clan_stipend` ($2.99) that `store.json` sells. VERIFIED.** The owner creating products from that table will ship a store where the two cheapest entry products show "Not sold here" (`economyUi.ts:110`). WORLDWIDE_AUDIT line 366 mentions them; SHIPPING does not.

**E09 — LOW — Regional pricing: one USD ladder, no low-income tiering; the under-18 cap and spend analytics use catalog USD. VERIFIED.** `store.json currency: USD`; `purchases.ts` caps by `cents(product.usd)`; proceeds in local currency are not modeled. Store-formatted prices are shown (GLOBAL-007 code fix). With Apple/Google tier equalization a $0.99 tier is ₹89 / R$5.90; there is no cheaper regional SKU or regional stipend. Decide whether to use store price templates (owner) and record the policy.

**E10 — LOW — League win bonuses are flat, not Keep-scaled. VERIFIED.** `ranks.json winBonus` 1k…80k coins vs raid loot 10k (Keep 3) … 14 M (Keep 15): meaningful at Keeps 3–5, noise from Keep 8. `curves.test` scales league *chests* only. Scale with `rewardScaleByHall` or drop.

## B. Jade faucets, sinks and exploits

**E11 — HIGH — Device (non-server) villages have zero clock-tamper protection, and an imported village keeps all clock-skipped progress. VERIFIED.** `SystemClock` = `Date.now()`; no backward/forward detection anywhere (`grep` of app/controller/persist). `villageImport.ts` header admits "the server cannot tell an edited save from real offline play"; `importProblem` checks only legality (Keep limits, storage, research caps, timers ≤ 30 d), `checkProvenance` holds Jade above `freeJadeAllowance(keep)` = 2,000 + 1,000·(keep−1) (16,000 at Keep 15). A player who stays offline, jumps the phone clock a day at a time (bot raids cap at 2/day thanks to the shield), reaches Keep 10–12 with capped research and ~5–10k Jade from chests/war chests (E12) in an afternoon, then connects, imports and raids real players. The roadmap already flags "trusted-local vs competitive" as an owner decision; for a competitive launch: cap the importable Keep (e.g. ≤ 6), or create every online village server-side at first contact and treat the device village as a backup.

**E12 — HIGH — The offline bot Alliance War pays Jade every week with no participation, for every week missed, unbounded. VERIFIED.** `village.ts advance()` → `settleRaids` (`social/groveRaid.ts:162-189`) loops `for (week = settledWeek+1; end <= now; …)` and pays `rewards.loss` (10 Jade + 40k×scale) even with zero attacks; bot-vs-bot star odds are symmetric (`starsFor`), so EV ≈ 0.45·40 + 0.1·25 + 0.45·10 ≈ **25 Jade/week**, and a returning player collects every missed week at once (52 weeks ≈ 1,300 Jade + storage-filling resources). Combined with E11 this is the main offline Jade mint. Suggest: require ≥ 1 attack for the chest, settle at most the last 1–2 weeks, and pay the loss chest in resources only.

**E13 — MEDIUM — Daily Gang Wars against a generated Gang are a 15 Jade/day faucet with Keep-scaled resources. SUSPECTED (structure verified, win rate not measured).** `social.json gangWar`: win 15 Jade + 60k×scale, one war per Gang at a time (`gangWars.ts:146`), 24 h per war, a bot Gang after 3 minutes (`botAfterMinutes`), Gang Rating untouched by bots, honor never moves. Bot results use `starOdds` (EV ~1.55 stars/attack); a 4-friend Gang that reliably beats that earns ~450 Jade/month plus ~one raid's worth of resources each per day with no honor risk. Suggest lower bot-war chests (e.g. half, no Jade) or a weekly cap on bot-paired wars.

**E14 — MEDIUM — Online Alliance War chests are free-rider friendly. VERIFIED.** `server/core/groveRaid.ts:146-173`: eligibility = "in the Grove when the raid started"; no attack required; the loss chest is 25 % of the win chest for everyone. Cooperation does not beat defection individually (your two attacks cost an army; the chest comes anyway). Suggest chest tier by own attacks used, or a small per-attack bonus.

**E15 — MEDIUM — Online "Next" is a client-honoured coin fee; `POST /v1/match` itself is free (30/min). SUSPECTED.** `app.ts:72-86` dispatches `scout(paid)` locally, then calls `port.match()`; `pvp.ts:261-321` voids the previous unstarted ticket and issues a new one without checking that a scout fee was replayed. A modified web client (or anyone calling the API) can reroll real players' bases up to 30×/min at no cost to cherry-pick loot. Low money impact (coins), real fairness impact. Tie match() to a replayed paid scout, or charge the fee server-side.

**E16 — LOW — Abandoning an offline battle keeps the scouted opponent and costs no honor: a free probe. VERIFIED.** `village.ts:687-695` (`abandonBattle`) spends only deployed warriors and does not clear `s.scout`; `battleEnd` does. A player can drop one Shinobi, watch the defenses fire, abandon, and attack the same base with the full army. Online, `attack-commit` turns an unreported start into a loss, so this is offline-only.

**E17 — LOW — Honor dumping is cheap (−20 per one-warrior loss) but bounded by the power floor. VERIFIED.** `attackLoss base 20`; floor `attackerStrength − 0.25` (`opponentFor`) means you can at best reach bases ~1 Keep below at 0.8 loot (`lootPenaltyByHallDiff[1]`). Not worth exploiting; fine.

**E18 — LOW — Daily chest outcomes are predictable from the village seed and the seed is choosable for device villages. VERIFIED.** `daily.ts chestOutcome(seed, day)`; a new offline village gets a new seed (`createNewVillage`), and `?debug` exposes `window.tk`, so one can re-create villages until future days roll "Great Supply Cart" (10 Jade + 80k×scale, 5 %). Server-born villages take the server seed. Minor; worth mixing in a server secret for imported villages.

**E19 — LOW — Jade sinks are thin: builders (250/500/1000), 3 skins (200–300), skips, top-ups, nothing else. VERIFIED.** The model player (`playerModel.ts`) never spends Jade and still reaches Keep 15 at day 121 (regular) with 2,600 Jade banked (`villageImport.ts:46-52`). No shield purchase, no cosmetic rotation, no research/training skip bundles, no "4th research lane". Payers have little to buy except time at E05 prices.

**E20 — LOW — Refund abuse is handled (3 refunds / 90 d pause, negative Jade debt), under-18 cap is atomic, no paid random items, "ends in" timers honest, pass sale closed in last 4 days. VERIFIED.** `purchases.ts:33-36,86-93`, `village.ts revokePurchase`, `events.ts` schedule-only windows, `offers.ts` fixed `expiresAt`, `daily.json` odds shown. Nothing to fix; keep.

## C. Economy integrity

**E21 — MEDIUM — Everything that pays Jade on a device village is client-trusted until import, and the import allowance is generous. VERIFIED.** `grantPurchase` without `verified` (controller.ts:311-314) when `purchaseVerifier()` is null (no server, or server without that store), chests, stipend claims, achievements, trials, bounty, war chests all run on the device. Import holds only Jade above 2k–16k. ## D. Pacing, difficulty, walls

**E23 — MEDIUM — The Keep is not the wall; collectors, camps and research are, and the Keep timers alone are only 35.7 days. VERIFIED (computed).** Sum of Keep `buildSeconds` 1→15 = 857.6 h = 35.7 d; at maxed collectors each Keep's cost is 0.8–2.3 days of income (`econ.mjs`), yet the regular model needs 121 d and casual 266 d. The remaining time is collector/storage/camp timers (7-day caps from L15+, `curves.test` allows ≤ 7 d) and research: capping the bot-mix army at Keep 15 costs 290 M Provisions (6.8 d of maxed income) and 8.1 lane-days; at Keep 14 it is 119 M. Offers are keyed to "first long wait" and "builders busy", not to these walls (collector L15+ at 7 days each is where a timer offer would land).

**E24 — MEDIUM — Keep 3 is an early dip right in the D1–D3 window. VERIFIED (generated CAMPAIGN.md).** Regular: Keep 3 avg 0.88 stars, 0 % three-star, 45 % destruction; casual explorer: **0.00 stars over 6 raids**, 34 % destruction. Keep 2 gives 2.67 stars, so the second day of play feels like a cliff. Cause: `troopLevelCap` stays 2 at Keep 3 while opponents are Keep 2.3–2.5 with the new tier's buildings. Consider a Keep-3-specific slack (`researchFloorKeeps` or `powerSlack` by Keep).

**E25 — MEDIUM — Late-game failure loop is still open for casual explorers and the regular model never three-stars at Keep 15. VERIFIED (CAMPAIGN.md).** Regular Keep 12: 0.84 stars / 2 % three-star; Keep 15: 1.40 / 0 %; casual explorer Keeps 9–14: 0.14–0.33 stars, honor collapses 1,235 → 450. `army-lab.test` gates "no army over 92 % / 60 % three-star" but has no *lower* gate on the model player's win rate; add one (e.g. regular ≥ 1.5 stars at every Keep).

**E26 — LOW — Whether defense is worth building is not measured anywhere. VERIFIED.** `campaign.ts` reports only the count of raids against the village; the defender policy test checks pace to Keep 6, not losses. With losses capped at 20 % of storage / `storageLootCapByHall`, a 12 h shield after any ≥ 30 % raid, and the held-defense reward at 5 % of min loot, the rational player probably builds minimal defense. Add stolen-resource and honor-loss columns to the campaign.

**E27 — LOW — Research-done tasks and "clear 2 new Trial tiers/week" get infeasible late; `taskEligible` handles research/upgrades/obstacles but not Trials. VERIFIED.** `pass.ts taskEligible` returns `true` for `trialsCleared`; from tier 60 defenses grow +6 %/tier (`trials.json`) so weekly `w_trials: 2` becomes unreachable for most; destruction tasks substitute only for star/win tasks.

**E28 — LOW — Daily boundaries are UTC (chest, stipend, tasks, honor-loss cap). VERIFIED.** `pass.ts dayIndex` uses `pass.epoch` in UTC; for the Americas the "day" resets at 4–7 pm local. Not an exploit, but a retention/UX note (and the model player's "daily" is UTC too).

**E29 — LOW — Cold start works but reads as empty. VERIFIED.** With no players, every online raid is labelled "Generated rival (no player fitted your rank)" (`app.ts:93`), Gang Wars go to a generated Gang after 3 minutes, league chests pay by own honor regardless of others. Honest and functional; consider softer copy and NPC rival clans (already an owner item).

**E30 — LOW — Shadow Trials rewards vs. army cost roughly break even and are once-per-tier; alternates only after a failure. VERIFIED.** `trialReward` = 25 % of the hall's min loot × (1 + 0.1·log₂ t), army refill ≈ 25 % of avg loot (`troops.mjs`); 5 Jade every 10 tiers. Not farmable. Difficulty spikes handled by median-of-5 and `layoutPower`; the "ironclad every 5th" tiers still double wall HP on top.

**E31 — INFO — Refill/loot ratio is a flat ~4 from Keep 5 (`troops.mjs`), training ≤ 1.6 h at a maxed Dojo, raid-economy is the intended loop; collectors at Keep 15 (1.78 M/h, 8 h cap) out-earn raiding, so late raids are optional.** VERIFIED (computed).

## Big-picture assessment
The economy is unusually honest and well-guarded against the classic dark patterns (no loot boxes, honest timers, atomic minor cap, refund debt, server-replayed economy for server villages). But as a *business* it is under-monetized: free Jade (~540/month passive) matches the $9.99 pack, the monthly product ($2.99 → 600 Jade) is 3× better than any pack, the only big sink (skips) is priced at ~2× genre norms, the first-purchase offer is structurally lost for most players, and offers never reach undeclared-age or offline players. The main competitive-integrity hole is the device→server bridge (clock skipping + unbounded offline war chests, then import). The model player can reach Keep 15 in 121 days without spending a single Jade, which means there is no designed reason to pay beyond impatience, and impatience is priced so that free Jade covers it.

Top 5 changes:
1. Fix the offer funnel: ask age at tutorial end, make `first_long_wait` re-armable, let Starter outrank Bundle (E01, E02).
2. Rebalance Jade: cut passive faucets (bot-war chest needs an attack and settles ≤ 2 weeks; bot Gang Wars no Jade; streak Jade weekly not daily) and lower skip prices ~40 % so paying for time is the rational sink (E05, E12, E13, E19).
3. Reprice the stipend (≈ 10–12 Jade/day or login-gated) so it is the entry product, not the ceiling (E03).
4. Close the device→server bridge before a competitive launch: cap importable Keep or create online villages server-side at first contact (E11, E21).
5. Add retention gates to the balance tests: a lower bound on model-player stars per Keep (fix the Keep 3 dip and the Keep 12–15 slump) and stolen-resource/defense metrics in the campaign (E24–E26).

Secondary: tie `/v1/match` to a replayed paid scout (E15), scale league win bonuses (E10), add the two missing products to `docs/SHIPPING.md` 3b (E08), and decide a regional pricing policy (E09).
