# Bayanan shago da Hausa — Kagemura: Ninja Clans

Rubutun da aka shirya don App Store da Google Play (ha), da harshe da kalmomi iri ɗaya da na wasan (`locales/ha/TERMS.md`). Kowane shago ne ke nuna farashi da allon saye. Saka bayanan shago da hotunan allo aiki ne na lokacin fitarwa, ba ɓangare na ginin cikin gida ba. Ba a saka wannan rubutu a dashbod na kowane shago ba tukuna, kuma ɗan asalin harshen Hausa bai yi bitarsa ba tukuna. `locales/languages.json` ya nuna Hausa a matsayin `appStore: false`: App Store Connect ba ya bayar da wannan harshe don bayanan shago, don haka layukan App Store na gaba ne kawai.

- Suna: **Kagemura: Ninja Clans**
- Ƙaramin taken App Store: **Ɓoyayyen ƙauyen ninja**
- Gajeren bayanin Google Play: **Ku gina ɓoyayyen ƙauye, ku horar da mayaƙa, ku kai wa abokan hamayya hari**
- Rubutun talla: **Ku jagoranci danginku cikin Gwaje-gwajen Inuwa marasa ƙarshe. Hayaƙi, zagon ƙasa da dabara sun fi ƙarfi kawai.**
- Kalmomin bincike: `ninja,shinobi,dangi,ƙauye,dabara,hari,tsaro,hasumiya,fada,yaƙi,ƙawance,hayaƙi`

## Bayani

Ku jagoranci dangin ninja, ku gina ɓoyayyen ƙauye a kan duwatsu, ku horar da nau'o'in mayaƙa hamsin, ku leƙi abokan hamayya sannan ku kai musu hari da hayaƙi da zagon ƙasa.

**Ku gina ɓoyayyen ƙauye**

- Ku ɗaga Fadar Dangi daga mataki 1 zuwa 15 don buɗe gine-gine, katanga da gine-ginen tsaro.
- Kowane gini yana da aikinsa: Masana'antar Kuɗi, Gonar Matakai, Baitulmali, Dojon Horo, hasumiyoyi da tarkuna.
- Ku tsara tsaronku ta yadda mahara za su ratsa ta cikinsa.

**Ku horar da nau'o'in mayaƙa hamsin**

- Daga Shinobi da masu jifan kunai zuwa mahara masu filafilo, Masu Zagon Ƙasa da masu harba manyan bindigogin kewaye, kowanne da aikinsa.
- Ku yi bincike a Dojon Horo don mayaƙanku su ƙara ƙarfi.

**Ku tsara, sannan ku kai hari**

- Mayaƙa suna shiga ne ta Wuraren Kutse kaɗai. Ku leƙi hanyoyin da hasumiyoyin da ke kare su.
- Ku jefa Bam ɗin Hayaƙi lokacin da mayaƙanku suka shiga zangon hasumiya, ku tura Masu Zagon Ƙasa su tsayar da ginin tsaro.
- Harin da aka tsara da kyau yana cin runduna mafi girma.

**Ku yi wasa tare** (ta intanet)

- Ku shiga Ƙawance don hira, neman mayaƙa da aika su.
- Ku yi yaƙi a Yaƙin Ƙawance kowane mako.
- Ku ƙara abokai, ku ƙalubalance su a Fafatawar Abokantaka, ku kafa Gungu na mutum 4 a mafi yawa don Yaƙin Gungu.
- Ku hau rukunan daraja da Jerin Ƙarfi.

**Gwaje-gwajen Inuwa**

- Jerin fadodi marasa ƙarshe: kowane mataki ya fi na baya wuya, kuma kowane mataki yana ba da lada sau ɗaya kawai.

**Wasa na gaskiya**

- Akwatin yau da kullum kyauta yana nuna damar kowace kyauta kafin a buɗe shi; ba a sayar da komai na sa'a.
- Ana nuna abokan hamayya na kwamfuta a fili koyaushe.
- Ƴan wasa ƙasa da shekara 18 suna da iyakar kashe kuɗi; sanarwa sai waɗanda kuka zaɓa, kuma a kashe suke har sai kun kunna su.

Kagemura kyauta ce don saukewa da yin wasa. Kowane gini, mayaƙi, matakin Fada da yanayin wasa ana iya samunsu ba tare da biyan kuɗi ba, kuma babu talla. Jed, kuɗin musamman na wasan, ana samunsa kaɗan-kaɗan ta yin wasa (manufofi, rukunai, akwatin yau da kullum da layin kyauta na Takardar Kaka) kuma ana iya saye shi. Wasu abubuwa sai an saya: layin musamman na Takardar Kaka (ƙarin lada, gini cikin sauri a duk tsawon kaka da kamannin Fada "Bikin Fitilu") da kunshin Jed da albarkatu na ɗan lokaci.

## Hanyoyin fitarwa

Ku yi amfani da adireshin wasan da aka wallafa, ku ƙara `/ha/support.html`, `/ha/privacy.html` da `/ha/community.html`. Fom ɗin sabar yana karɓar `/account/delete?lang=ha`. Fassarorin Hausa na shafukan doka na Japan su ne `/ha/tokushoho.html` da `/ha/shikin-kessai.html`. Sharuɗɗan sabis da shafukan doka har yanzu daftari ne har sai mai gudanarwa ya cike bayanai ya fara aiki da su. Ƙa'idodin fitarwa suna cikin [RELEASE.md](../RELEASE.md).

## Bayanin sabon sigar

Kagemura yanzu tana da Hausa. Allunan, koyarwa, gine-gine, mayaƙa, faɗace-faɗace, sanarwa da shafukan taimako duk suna nuna Hausa. Ku canza harshe a Saituna → Harshe. Ƙauyenku da sunayen ƴan wasa suna nan yadda suke.

Hotunan allo na Hausa: `npm run build && npm run store:screenshots -- --language=ha` (yana buƙatar Playwright, Chromium da rubutun haruffa mai ɗauke da ɓ ɗ ƙ ƴ; ana iya zaɓar shirin da `CHROMIUM_PATH`)
