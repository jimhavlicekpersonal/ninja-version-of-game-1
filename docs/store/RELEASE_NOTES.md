# Release notes (P7-14)

The stores' "What's new" text for each version. The game shows the same text once in News (`src/presentation/ui/newsUi.ts` `WHATS_NEW`, key in `locales/en/ui.json`); `tests/presentation/ui/whats-new.test.ts` keeps them equal. Each language's text is in its `locales/<code>/ui.json` worksheet under the same key.

## 0.2.0

What's new: choose a larger text size, and turn screen shake and flashes down, in Settings. The stake picker shows how often your recent raids reached two and three stars. Shadow Trials climb more evenly, and raids at Keep 4 and Keep 15 meet fairer opponents.

## 0.1.0

What's new: send your squad in with one order, watch the fight up close or faster, and see what another entry point would have done. Every raid on your village now has a report, and you can test your defenses. New: a four-week event calendar with festivals, a weekly Gauntlet, daily contracts, achievements, Keep seals and looks to earn.
