# Silent first-time playtest (Beast Mode 2026-11-21 OPS-11)

The model player measures pacing; only people show where the game confuses them. Run this before wave A and after any
change to the first hour (OWN-24).

## Protocol

1. **Players:** at least 5 people who have never seen the game, a mix of ages (adults, or minors only with a parent's
   written consent and present), at least one who rarely plays mobile games and one who plays this genre.
2. **Setup:** a release build on their own phone where possible (else a supplied cheap Android). Screen and voice
   recording with their consent. A fresh install: no account, no save.
3. **Silence:** say only "please play as you would at home, and think aloud". No help, no hints, no answers. If they
   are stuck for 2 minutes, note the time and the screen, then say only "what would you try next?".
4. **Length:** 30 minutes, or until they stop.
5. **Notes while watching:** each stall (time, screen, what they tried), each misread (what they thought something
   meant), each moment of delight, and every purchase prompt they saw and how they reacted. Never prompt a purchase.
6. **Afterwards:** five questions: what is the game about; what were you trying to do at the end; what confused you;
   would you open it tomorrow and why; what would you tell a friend.

## Where the notes go

One file per session in a private folder the owner keeps (recordings never go into this repository). A summary line
