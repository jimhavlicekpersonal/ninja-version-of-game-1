# Kagemura privacy policy (draft for the owner to review and publish)

_Last updated: <date>. Publish at a public URL and link it in both store listings and in the game's Settings._

Kagemura ("the game") is made by <owner name / company> ("we"). Contact: <email address>.

## What we collect and why

- **Your online account.** When you play online, the game creates an account: a random player id, a secret token (we store only a one-way hash of it), and the player name you choose. Other players see your name.
- **Your game.** Your village, battles (replays are kept 30 days), honor, rankings, and the history of resources added or spent, so the game works on our server, other players can raid you fairly, and you can continue on another device.
- **Alliances.** Chat messages, troop requests and gifts you send in an Alliance. If someone reports a message, we keep its text until we have looked at it and for 90 days after our decision.
- **Sign-ins you link (optional).** If you link Sign in with Apple, Google, Game Center or Play Games, we keep only that service's id for you, never your email address or name.
- **Purchases.** For purchases in the game we keep the store's transaction id, the product, the price and whether it was refunded, to deliver each purchase once and to handle refunds. If you said you are under 18, we use this to keep you within the monthly spending limit.
- **Notifications (optional).** If you turn on notifications, we keep your device's notification token, the types you chose and your time-zone offset, so we do not notify you at night or too often.
- **Crash reports (optional).** If "Send crash reports" is on, the game sends error details (the error, where it happened, the game version and platform) to Sentry, without your player id or game data.
- **Network data.** Our server sees your IP address with each request. We use it to stop abuse (rate limits) and keep it only in short-lived counters and server logs.

We do not collect your email address, contacts, location or advertising id. There are no ads, no third-party tracking, and we never sell your data.

## Who processes it

Our hosting provider (server and database), Sentry (crash reports, only if switched on), Apple and Google (sign-in, purchases, notifications). They process data for us only.

## How long we keep it

As long as your account exists, except: battle replays 30 days; handled reports 90 days; server logs as set by our host. Purchase records are kept after an account is deleted, without your player id, because tax and refund rules require them.

## Your choices and rights

- **Delete your account** in the game (Settings → Online play → Account → Delete account) or on the web at <server>/account/delete. Deletion removes your account, village, chat messages and linked sign-ins from our server; replays of attacks you made stay in other players' defense logs as "Deleted player".
- Turn notifications and crash reports off at any time in Settings.
- Ask us for a copy of your data or to correct it: <email address>. If you are in the EU, UK or California you have these rights by law, and you can complain to your data protection authority.

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. Players under 18 have a monthly spending limit and see a reminder to ask a parent before buying.

## Changes

We will update this page and the date above, and tell players in the game about important changes.
