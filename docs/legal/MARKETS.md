# Country access matrix (OWNER, draft 2026-11-05)

Worldwide audit GLOBAL-006 (markets) and recommendation 10: keep a country-by-country matrix of what each market requires, what this repository already does, and what only the owner can do. Selecting a territory in App Store Connect or Play Console does not make the game legally available there. Statements of law are from knowledge plus the searches cited; **[recheck]** means confirm the current official text before relying on it. Nothing here is legal advice.

"Fix release" means the build with the neutral age screen (under 13 / 13–17 / adult before online play), no free-text chat under 13, private discovery defaults for 13–17, moderation notices, the deletion code, Sign in with Apple revocation, and offers that never open on their own for under-18 players (`minAccountHours` ≥ 24, `heavy_defense_loss` removed). Ship to child-sensitive markets only with that build or later.

Related: `docs/store/PRIVACY.md` (store declarations), `docs/store/PRIVACY_POLICY.md` (pages), `docs/legal/ONLINE_SAFETY_UK.md`, `docs/legal/JAPAN.md`.

## Product facts the matrix relies on

- Free to play, no ads, no tracking; purchases: Jade packs, offers (Jade + resources), Season Scroll (consumable each season). **Nothing random is sold**; the free daily chest shows odds.
- Online: player names, Alliance chat, Gang chat (2–4 friends), friends, duels, wars. No one-to-one DMs, images, voice or links preview (typed URLs are not blocked yet).
- Age: self-declared bands (fix release); under-18 monthly cap; no age verification.
- English only today (`locales/` not read by the game yet, audit R05).
- Server region and processors: owner choice (`hosting`, `crashReportRegion` in `legal-config.js`).

## App Store and Google Play territory notes

- **App Store Connect → Pricing and Availability:** pick territories explicitly; leave out China mainland (no licence yet; the owner pursues it through a licensed Chinese publisher, decision 2026-11-13) and Vietnam (no licence) for now. EU storefronts need the DSA trader declaration first. Korea and Brazil use regional age-rating values shown with the global answers.
- **Play Console → Countries/regions:** Play does not serve mainland China. EU: trader status under Policy → App content. Korea: IARC gives GRAC. Brazil: ClassInd via IARC. Target audience: 13+ (see `PRIVACY.md`); adding under-13 brings the Families policy.
- **Prices:** both stores convert prices; the game shows only store prices (GLOBAL-007). Check storefront prices and taxes per country before launch (not tested on real storefronts).
- **Languages:** English only today. Listing English-only in non-English markets is allowed but limits reach; translated rules and notices are needed for moderation in those languages (R05/R06).
