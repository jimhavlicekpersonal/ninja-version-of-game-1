## How the UI talks to the game

- **Read** state from `GameController.state` and pure functions in `src/gameplay/sim/*` (quotes, previews, scout reports, trial info).
- **Change** state only by sending commands: `controller.dispatch({ type: ... })`, or `BattleSession.tryDeploy(id, x, y)` in battle. Abilities such as Smoke Bomb use the same call with the ability id.
- Backend only through contract files (`compartments.json`): `Connection`, `GroveSource`, `Analytics`, `SaveStore`.
- Never put game rules or numbers in UI code. Ask the GAME THEORY AI in `docs/game-theory/REQUESTS.md`.

## Device targets and UX rules (from the original spec)

- Windows 11 laptop (mouse, wheel, Esc/Enter), Pixel 7 Chrome, iPhone Safari. Touch first: drag to pan, pinch to zoom, tap. Respect safe areas; portrait and landscape; check 360×640, 390×844 and 844×390.
- Readable silhouettes and clear combat on a phone: smoke shows its exact area and time left; jammed defenses dim with a jam marker and recovery ring; immune defenses show a thin ring.
- Generated or simulated opponents are always labelled ("Practice target", "Generated rival (offline)", "(simulated raider)", "Shadow Trial"). Never present them as real players.
- Real prices appear only in the Store and its confirm step. Every purchase has a clear confirm step, and no buy button sits where a stray tap lands. Under-18 spending cap and confirmation stay.
- The tutorial (`ui/tutorial.ts`) keeps nine steps: saves store the step index (re-audit design item 3 changed what steps ask, never their number or order: step 1 is place a Coin Mint, then Collect; step 4 is Dojo, Courtyard and a Kunai Tower; the wait-or-Jade steps show the real time left). A step points only at its own building's sheet (`.sheet[data-type="…"]`) or at the building on the map (`render/anchors.ts`, registered by VillageScene), never at a button under an open dialog; the bubble sits under the top bar and clear of the rail, and at the bottom while a sheet or dialog is open. It hides during battles; the first raid gets a one-line coach in the battle HUD instead (`BattleHudDeps.coach`, set in `app.ts` while the tutorial is not done): tap a glowing ring, send the rest through another, smoke them under fire.
- Battle start (re-audit design item 1): `BattleScene.fitEntry` frames every open Infiltration Point inside the area the HUD leaves free (`BattleHooks.safeArea`, measured by `battleHud`), down to `FIT_FLOOR` (0.14); points still outside it get an edge chevron (letter + space left) that pans to them (`BattleView.showPoint`). A tap within `SNAP_CSS_PX` of an open ring deploys on the nearest free tile inside it (warriors only; smoke goes anywhere). Toasts and the coach share one line that the fit keeps free (under the top cards, or between them sideways). The scout report is a drawer: one chip row (`N points · M defenses ▸` and a chip per point that highlights its path, `BattleView.highlightPath`); tips naming a warrior the player didn't bring are left out. Scouting buttons are a 3-column grid. Measure with Playwright at 320×568, 360×640, 390×844 and 844×390 (point screen positions and real taps).
- Progressive disclosure (re-audit design item 4): the rail shows Daily and Store first; Events (with its countdown pill), Pass, Goals and Alliance after the tutorial or Keep 2; Trials after the first raid; Friends while online. It folds (`tk.railCollapsed`). The Keep-unlock preview is a one-line chip the player can dismiss per Keep level; the full list is on the Keep's sheet. Army filters appear from 5 unlocked warriors; the Codex button sits beside the Army tabs.
- Art the HUD may not import (only art contract files are open to GUI): `ui/feedback.ts` `provideUiArt`, called by `main.ts` with the Sensei portrait and the chest reveal; haptics come from the `audio/sound.ts` contract. Result-screen feel (sound + haptics) goes through `celebrate`.
