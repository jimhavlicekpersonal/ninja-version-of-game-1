## What lives here

- `ninja.ts`: all code-drawn art. Buildings are painted per level (`paintBuildingLevel`, key `building.<type>.lv<level>`, on first use): the Keep has six looks and defenses five (`tierFor` explicit maps), other buildings three (thirds), and every level inside a tier adds a small change (a stone lantern on the plate per step, a taller plinth, wall coping colour, more spikes or lanterns; a gold kerb at the top level). Roof materials tell families apart (`roof({ material })`: thatch producers, kawara tile stores and military, cedar shingle → tile → copper for defenses and the Keep). Banners carry the clan's kamon set (`kamon()`: kunai, peak, arrows, moon, sheaf, wave, hammer); `crest()` is three straight kunai in a ring. Troops are chibi ninja (`ninja()`, facing right, feet at y = 0) with role head shapes (`hat`: kasa, kabuto `helm`, `wrap`, `hood`, `topknot`) and pose fields. Also projectiles, ruins by family (`paintRuins`), village-life sprites (`paintBird`, `paintVillager`, `paintStreamer`), smoke, the jam marker and other effects. Registries: `buildingPainters`, `troopArt`, `projectileArt`.
- `warriorIcons.ts`: one distinct badge per roster warrior (`icon.warrior.<id>`): role-coloured disc, tier ring, chibi ninja and a unique signature prop. `paintWarriorFigure` draws the figure with its role silhouette (`ROLE_STYLE`: blades lean, throwers wear a wide kasa, guardians a kabuto and shoulder plates, saboteurs crouch with a knotted head cloth and tool belt, infiltrators creep low in a spray of earth, mystics a robe and topknot with a glow, siege crews a boxy frame, shadows long hood tails and glowing eyes); the battle sprites (`warriorSpriteArt`, registered into `troopArt` when this module loads) use the same figure, guardians 1.22× and tiers 4–5 1.15× with an aura. `ROLE_RING` and `roleOfTroop` give the battle ground-ring colour. `tests/presentation/art/warriorIcons.test.ts` checks all 50 exist and draw different pictures.
- `registerWarriorArt` accepts catalog metadata from rendering without importing game data into art: it adds beast companions (`troop.<warriorId>.companion`) and a template sprite for any warrior `warriorIcons.ts` does not cover. Original six texture keys remain unchanged.
- `chest.ts` (daily chest SVG and the honest `revealChest`), `portraits.ts` (Sensei Hoshi, `senseiPortrait(expression)`): DOM art for the HUD, re-exported from `ninja.ts`. What the GUI wires is listed in `docs/art/REQUESTS.md`.
- `ground.ts`: the mountain plateau rising out of a sea of clouds (crags, cloud banks, shrub rim, worn paths and stepping stones, a stream and waterfall on the front-left cliff, sakura/maple/pine trees on the back rim, warm lanterns at the front corner), the mist wisps (`water.wave`), the dusk backdrop, and the parallax mountain bands and fog (`paintRidge`, `paintFogBand`; placed by `render/fx.ts` `addScenery`). `toolkit.ts`: colour and shading helpers.
- Materials: the shared helpers (`block`, `roof`, `stoneBase`, `storey`, `plate`) light faces from the upper left with gradients, rim light and eave shadows, so a new building gets depth for free. Each building also shows its job (see the root `CLAUDE.md`, Art).
- App icon and splash screens: `tools/brand/brand.mjs` (`npm run brand`) renders the Kagemura mark (the Keep against a vermilion sun over the cloud sea) to every PWA, iOS and Android size; master SVGs in `docs/art/brand/`.
- `../audio/sound.ts`: synthesized SFX and music. Plucked koto and shamisen are Karplus-Strong strings rendered once per pitch into cached buffers; taiko drums, a wooden clack for taps, a shakuhachi-like breath for smoke, a victory sting, and `starSlam`, `collapse`, `perfect` for battles. No audio files, no square-wave chiptune.
- `../audio/haptics.ts` (re-exported from `sound.ts`): `haptic('light' | 'medium' | 'heavy' | 'success' | 'selection')` through `@capacitor/haptics` in the apps; on the web only the strong moments vibrate briefly where `navigator.vibrate` exists (never on iOS Safari); off with `setHapticsEnabled(false)` (localStorage `tk.haptics`).
- `public/art/manifest.json`: real image files, keyed by asset key. Loaded by `render/artManifest.ts`. A listed image replaces the code art with the same key.

## Rules

- Imports: only within `art/`, plus `render/iso.ts` for tile sizes (enforced by `tests/architecture/boundaries.test.ts`).
- Keys are the contract (`docs/art/ASSET_KEYS.md`). Never rename a key. To ask for a new key or animation, write to `docs/art/REQUESTS.md`.
- Original IP only: no franchise look-alikes, real crests, religious symbols or Clash of Clans resemblance. The clan crest is three kunai in a ring (`crest()`).
- Readability on a phone beats detail: silhouettes at 40 px, icons at 48 px.
- Painters are resolution-independent: `render/assets.ts` draws them at 2.5x (buildings) and 3x (warriors, badges) in high quality and at 1.5x and 2x in low quality (`render/quality.ts`), and paints each building level only when it is first shown (both profiles). Check new art at both (`?quality=low`); thin lines and small marks must still read at 1.5x. Emulated texture sums (`npm run perf:lowend -- --quality`): a new village holds ~115 MB at high (buildings 65, island 35) and ~24 MB at low; low with every tier painted would be ~46 MB.
- Preview with `npm run art` (gallery of every texture and tier). Check in game with `npm run dev`.
- Finish with `npm test` and `npm run build`.
